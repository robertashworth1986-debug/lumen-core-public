## Non-Negotiable Drafting Rules

- Use measured values with dataset, split, comparator, date, and artifact reference.
- Label historical route selection as exploratory and prospective evidence as pending until settled.
- Do not convert internal reproducibility into external validation.
- Do not use `unbeatable`, `proven`, `government-grade`, `enterprise-ready`, or valuation language without a matching accepted gate.
- Preserve failed gates and invalid LLM outputs.
- Cite primary sources for state-of-the-art and official requirements.
- Keep marketing material out of Volume 2.
