## Volume 7 Answer Domains

The corporate official must answer the live DSIP wording. The eight current
question domains are:

1. malign foreign talent recruitment programs involving an owner or covered individual;
2. a parent, joint venture, or subsidiary based in or funded by a foreign country of concern;
3. current or pending contractual, financial, business, or joint-venture-like arrangements with a foreign state-owned enterprise or foreign entity;
4. whole ownership in a foreign country;
5. venture-capital or institutional investment and relevant foreign affiliations of investing-entity leadership;
6. technology licensing or intellectual-property sales or transfers to a foreign country of concern during the previous five years;
7. related foreign, offshore, or non-U.S. entities; and
8. an owner, officer, or covered individual's foreign affiliation with a research institution in a foreign country of concern.

No answer is prefilled or certified by this memo. The portal record and current
