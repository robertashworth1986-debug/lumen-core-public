## Shared Background Technology

The following capabilities appear across packages and should be described as
pre-existing, privately developed LumenCore background technology unless a
final funding-history review establishes otherwise:

- deterministic configuration and replay;
- source adapters and provenance labeling;
- baseline-versus-candidate evaluation and holdout controls;
- constrained routing, abstention, and human approval patterns;
- SHA-256 manifests, result receipts, and negative-result retention;
- test harnesses, dashboards, and reviewer-facing evidence summaries.

An award may fund proposal-specific configuration, integration, extension,
evaluation, documentation, and transition work. It must not rebill the same
background implementation effort or represent the background stack as newly
created under multiple awards.
