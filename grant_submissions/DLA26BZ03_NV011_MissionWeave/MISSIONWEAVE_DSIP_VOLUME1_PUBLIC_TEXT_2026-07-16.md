# MissionWeave DSIP Volume 1 Public Text

Title: MissionWeave: A Bounded Organizational Digital Twin for Mission-Readiness Decisions

Status: Nonproprietary DSIP-entry candidate; corporate-official review required before submission.

## Technical Abstract

MissionWeave is a bounded, event-driven digital-twin proof of concept for testing how work moves through a mission process under surge, absence, degraded-system, and human-machine teaming scenarios. Phase I will first rank at least three candidate use cases using mission relevance, data feasibility, security and privacy burden, decision value, and transition potential. It will then implement one unclassified representative process, Critical Supply Exception Triage and Disposition, unless a DLA Component selects a better bounded process during kickoff.

The proof of concept will maintain a versioned process graph connecting cases, stages, roles, skills, systems, deadlines, constraints, and human approvals. A source registry will distinguish observed, approved-assumption, public, and synthetic records. Frozen current-state, fixed-role FIFO, and cross-trained FIFO comparators will be replayed under identical scenarios. Candidate interventions will be evaluated on on-time critical disposition, completion, cycle time, backlog, bottleneck exposure, workload concentration, recommendation stability, and abstention behavior. Every result will carry configuration, source, software, and output hashes so an authorized reviewer can reproduce it.

Existing generated-workflow evidence supports feasibility only: a policy selected on 16 development scenarios was held fixed across 30 disjoint validation seeds in five conditions. It improved mean on-time rate relative to cross-trained FIFO in that generated model, but some seeds were worse and absolute performance remained poor under combined stress. No DLA data, workforce productivity, causal impact, operational readiness, fairness validation, or 10x improvement is claimed.

Phase I will deliver a ranked use-case register, data acquisition and synthetic-data plan, bounded proof of concept, evaluation scorecard, negative-result register, responsible-use controls, business case, and milestone-based MVP roadmap. Recommendations remain advisory and require human approval; employment, contract-award, protected-trait, classified, and operational-command decisions are out of scope.

## Anticipated Benefits

For DLA, MissionWeave would provide a disciplined way to compare workforce and process interventions before operational adoption. Leaders could see where capacity, skill coverage, handoffs, system degradation, or policy constraints limit timely mission work; compare staffing, routing, automation, and stop-doing options under the same frozen scenarios; and inspect assumptions, uncertainty, failure regions, and rollback conditions before making a decision. The Phase I business case will quantify value with approved inputs rather than asserting savings in advance.

The proposed approach supports DLA's need to identify high-impact digital-workforce-twin use cases, define credible data and synthetic-data paths, establish success criteria, and plan an MVP. Its process-bounded scope reduces the scientific and governance risk of claiming a complete causal twin of an organization. Human approval, minimal personnel data, explicit synthetic labeling, reproducible receipts, and abstention under unsupported conditions are built into the evaluation contract.

Commercial and public-sector adjacencies include industrial logistics, regulated workflow, field service, continuity planning, and other operations in which delays, rework, skill bottlenecks, or outages create measurable cost. A successful Phase II pilot could support fixed-price configuration and integration, annual software licensing, and validation/support services. No customer commitment, realized savings, or production deployment is claimed in Phase I.
