## Controlling Change

Amendment 01 states:

- TA1/TA2 requires CMMC Level 1.
- TA3 requires CMMC Level 2.
- The CAGE code associated with the proposal must have a valid, unexpired Final
  status at the applicable level recorded in SPRS to be eligible for award.
- A current annual CMMC affirmation by an authorized Affirming Official must be
  recorded in SPRS.
- The applicable status must be maintained throughout contract performance.
- Applicable requirements flow down to subcontractors that process, store, or
  transmit FCI or CUI on unclassified systems.

The amendment removes the original Section 3.3 lead-in that limited the stated
CMMC requirements to procurement-contract awards. Because the replacement
language is broader, the safe planning posture is to treat Final Level 1,
SPRS, CAGE linkage, and annual affirmation as TA1/TA2 award-eligibility gates
unless DARPA provides written clarification for the selected award instrument.
