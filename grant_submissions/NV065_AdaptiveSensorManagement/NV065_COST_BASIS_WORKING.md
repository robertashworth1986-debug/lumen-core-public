## Labor Assumption

The direct-labor planning basis uses a provisional $95/hour blended direct
rate:

- Base: about 1,000 direct labor hours.
- Option: about 560 direct labor hours.

The hours are intended to cover unclassified interface definition,
representative-model implementation, covariance-filter and latency measures,
policy comparison, evidence packaging, documentation, and transition planning.
Actual full-proposal budget categories must match the final performer
structure, employee/consultant treatment, and reviewed indirect-rate basis.

## Base Work Package

- Confirm unclassified sensor-tasking message boundary and operator-review
  constraints.
- Build a notional radar-resource model for SPS-48, SPQ-9B, MK-9
  Tracker/Illuminator, and SPY-6(V)3 using approved unclassified assumptions.
- Add covariance-filter, latency, and tasking-cost measures.
- Compare fixed-priority, uncertainty-first, marginal-contribution, and
  stress-aware reallocation policies.
- Produce explainable continue/release/reallocate recommendation objects.
- Freeze generated and representative-model evidence with manifests,
  scorecards, and reproducibility instructions.
- Draft Phase II security, SSDS-compatibility, data, and clearance transition
  plan.

## Option Work Package

- Harden representative-model assumptions and edge-case scenarios.
- Expand scenario families for dense raid, degradation, hostility shift,
  maneuver, clutter, and capacity stress.
- Conduct independent sensor-management/domain review.
- Refine the notional SSDS-facing recommendation schema and latency budget.
- Prepare final transition package and Phase II readiness plan.
