## Official Requirements Checked

Source: `https://seedfund.nsf.gov/apply/project-pitch/`

Checked on June 19, 2026:

- Project Pitch fields are Technology Innovation, Technical Objectives and
  Challenges, Market Opportunity, and Company and Team.
- Character limits are 3,500, 3,500, 1,750, and 1,750 respectively.
- NSF describes the pitch as a fit screen for technical innovation,
  intellectual/technical merit, broader impacts, and commercial potential.
- A declined pitch is a fit decision, not a judgment that the idea has no
  value.
- An invitation to submit a full proposal does not imply Phase I funding.
- Only one Project Pitch per submission deadline is allowed.
- A small business with a pending Project Pitch, open invitation, or full
  SBIR/STTR Phase I proposal under review must wait for a response before
  submitting another Project Pitch.
