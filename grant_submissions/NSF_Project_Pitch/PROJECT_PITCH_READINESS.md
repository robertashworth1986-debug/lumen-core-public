# NSF Project Pitch Readiness

Updated: June 13, 2026

## Status

- The NSF Project Pitch gate is currently open and rolling.
- A research-institution partner is not required for an NSF SBIR Project
  Pitch.
- SAM.gov and Research.gov readiness are not required to send the short pitch,
  but they will block an invited full proposal.

## Completed

- Four required narrative sections drafted.
- Legacy `333/673` router and universal harmonic-superiority claims removed.
- Leakage-resistant V7 negative results used to motivate abstention.
- Market entry is framed as paid evaluation pilots, not guaranteed savings.
- Team gaps and evidence boundaries are explicit.

## Before Portal Entry

1. Confirm the exact legal business name used for the NSF account.
2. Confirm the founder/PI name and title to use consistently.
3. Select the primary NSF technical topic.
4. Decide whether the first market should be energy/infrastructure or a
   narrower regulated-data segment.
5. Confirm that no substantially equivalent pitch is already under NSF review.
6. Recount the four portal text fields after pasting because portal handling
   of line breaks may change character counts.

## Full-Proposal Blockers If Invited

- Active SAM.gov registration and current expiration date.
- Research.gov organization and PI/AOR roles.
- Named consultants or subawardees and letters only where permitted.
- Defensible salary, indirect-cost, cloud-compute, and consultant bases.
- Customer discovery or pilot evidence.
- Data-management, cybersecurity, and commercialization detail.
