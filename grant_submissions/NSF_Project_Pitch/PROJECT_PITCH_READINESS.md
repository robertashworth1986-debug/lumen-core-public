## Before Portal Entry

1. Confirm the exact legal business name used for the NSF account.
2. Confirm the founder/PI name and title to use consistently.
3. Confirm that no substantially equivalent pitch is already under NSF review.
4. Confirm there is no open NSF invitation or full SBIR/STTR Phase I proposal
   under review for the same small business.
5. Recount the four portal text fields after pasting because portal handling
   of line breaks may change character counts.
6. Keep any customer/pilot language conditional unless a specific customer has
   given written permission to be named.

## Full-Proposal Blockers If Invited

- Active SAM.gov registration and current expiration date.
- Research.gov organization and PI/AOR roles.
- Named consultants or subawardees and letters only where permitted.
- Defensible salary, indirect-cost, cloud-compute, and consultant bases.
- Customer discovery or pilot evidence.
- Data-management, cybersecurity, and commercialization detail.
