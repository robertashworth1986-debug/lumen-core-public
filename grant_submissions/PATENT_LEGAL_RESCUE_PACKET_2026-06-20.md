## Pro Bono / Low-Cost Patent Help Targets

1. USPTO Patent Pro Bono Program
   - Source: https://www.uspto.gov/patents/basics/using-legal-services/pro-bono/patent-pro-bono-program
   - Why: USPTO describes this as a national network matching financially underresourced inventors and small businesses with volunteer patent attorneys and agents.
   - Key readiness items: income eligibility, knowledge of the patent system or provisional already filed, and ability to describe the invention.

2. USPTO Tennessee resources
   - Source: https://www.uspto.gov/about-us/uspto-office-locations/tennessee
   - Why: USPTO states Tennessee inventors are served by the Tennessee PATENTS Program and lists law school clinic routes.
   - Action: Apply through the Tennessee PATENTS / current regional successor path and ask for urgent non-provisional deadline review.

3. Tennessee PATENTS / Georgia PATENTS / Georgia Lawyers for the Arts path
   - Source: https://abcnashville.org/may20for20/
   - Why: Nashville Arts & Business Council states Tennessee PATENTS is now served through Georgia PATENTS by Georgia Lawyers for the Arts.
   - Action: Contact the program with the filing receipt, deadline, and a one-page invention summary.

4. USPTO Law School Clinic Certification Program
   - Source: https://www.uspto.gov/learning-and-resources/ip-policy/public-information-about-practitioners/law-school-clinic-1
   - Why: USPTO lists law school clinics that provide pro bono patent/trademark services to qualified clients; some accept clients nationwide.
   - Action: Contact clinics that list patent services and all-U.S. geographic eligibility.

5. Vanderbilt Intellectual Property and the Arts Clinic
   - Source: https://law.vanderbilt.edu/clinics-experiential-learning/ip-arts-clinic/
   - Why: Local Nashville IP clinic for intellectual property and arts/technology matters, though the public page emphasizes copyright, trademark, contracts, licensing, counseling, and related IP fields more than utility patents.
   - Action: Ask for triage/referral if they cannot handle the patent filing directly.
