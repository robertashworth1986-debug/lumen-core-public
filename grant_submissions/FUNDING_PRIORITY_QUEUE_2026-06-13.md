## Federal Contracts

Contracts are procurement, not grant-style idea funding. A competitive bid
normally needs a specific solicitation, NAICS alignment, representations and
certifications, a capability statement, price/cost basis, delivery schedule,
and evidence that the business can perform the statement of work.

Near-term contract work should target small scoped software deliverables:

- reproducible benchmark and validation services;
- streaming anomaly detection prototypes;
- evidence-chain and audit tooling;
- data-pipeline reliability and monitoring; and
- human-approved agent workflow integration.

Do not bid on hardware, classified, clinical, or institutional trading
performance requirements that the current company cannot yet substantiate.
