## Proposed Title

Proof-to-Pilot Data Validation Layer for AI-Enabled Transportation Systems Management and Operations

## Core Fit

LumenCore fits the TSMO Data Initiative through:

- Source-quality gates for operational transportation data.
- Baseline-vs-candidate replay for AI/ML use cases.
- Evidence manifests for model claims.
- Prioritized use-case evaluation.
- Prototype algorithm/model packaging.
- Reviewer-facing proof cards.
- Human-in-the-loop pilot gates.

## Working Technical Concept

LumenCore would develop a repeatable evaluation layer for AI TSMO use cases:

1. Identify data barriers
   - Data availability, latency, quality, provenance, gaps, stale feeds, inconsistent schemas, and unclear ownership.

2. Prioritize use cases
   - Rank potential AI TSMO use cases by data readiness, safety impact, operational value, pilot feasibility, and validation burden.

3. Build prototype models
   - Produce at least two prototype AI/ML models or decision-support workflows where the solicitation allows.
   - Keep first prototypes focused on data-quality-aware traffic operations, anomaly review, incident/queue pattern detection, or operations decision support.

4. Compare against baselines
   - Lock an incumbent baseline for each use case.
   - Compare candidate outputs using accepted metrics and preserved source records.

5. Package evidence
   - Produce proof manifests, uncertainty notes, negative-result records, and pilot recommendations.

## Candidate Use Cases

### Use Case A: Data Readiness And Anomaly Review For Operations Feeds

Purpose:

- Detect stale, missing, conflicting, or low-quality operational data before AI tools use it.

LumenCore role:

- Source inventory, quality scoring, and proof-card summary of feed trust state.

Potential metrics:

- Missingness.
- Staleness.
- Conflict rate.
- Review burden.
- Detection lead time.

### Use Case B: Baseline-Compared AI Incident / Congestion Pattern Review

Purpose:

- Evaluate whether an AI model improves review triage against an accepted non-AI or incumbent baseline.

LumenCore role:

- Replay candidate outputs against historical windows with frozen metrics.

Potential metrics:

- Precision/recall where labels exist.
- Alert burden.
- False-review burden.
- Time-to-review.
- Confidence calibration.

### Use Case C: Multi-Source TSMO Evidence Manifest

Purpose:

- Convert fragmented transportation operations feeds into auditable decision records for pilot readiness.

LumenCore role:

- Manifest schema, hash record, reviewer card, and pilot gate.

Potential metrics:

- Source coverage.
- Data lineage completeness.
- Reproducibility rate.
- Reviewer approval readiness.
