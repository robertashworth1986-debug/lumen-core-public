## Proposed Phase I Title

MissionWeave: Evidence-Bounded Organizational Digital Twin for AI-Augmented Surge Readiness and Process Optimization

## One-Sentence Concept

LumenCore will prototype an evidence-bounded organizational digital twin that models mission workflows, generates synthetic surge scenarios, compares AI-augmented process changes against locked baselines, and packages results into reviewer-ready proof manifests for DLA decision support.

## Phase I Technical Objectives

1. Define the organizational workflow model
   - Represent roles, tasks, queues, handoffs, constraints, and mission readiness indicators.
   - Separate measured inputs, assumptions, and synthetic scenario parameters.

2. Build synthetic scenario generator
   - Generate surge, staffing, process-friction, supply-chain disruption, and AI-assistance scenarios.
   - Preserve seed, configuration, and run metadata for replay.

3. Implement baseline-vs-candidate evaluation
   - Lock incumbent process baselines before testing AI-augmented changes.
   - Compare throughput, delay, rework, review burden, escalation, and readiness proxies.

4. Produce evidence manifests
   - Record source, baseline, candidate, metric, result, uncertainty, and failure modes.
   - Preserve negative and inconclusive outcomes.

5. Deliver operator-facing proof cards
   - Translate technical runs into decision records for leadership review.
   - Identify what should advance to Phase II validation, what needs data, and what should stop.
