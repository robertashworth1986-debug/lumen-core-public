## Diligence Questions

### equity_vesting

- Question: Is the 20% equity fixed at signing, or does it vest against named milestones?
- Why it matters: Milestone vesting protects the company if deliverables, intros, or validation support do not happen.

### cash_budget

- Question: What exact cash does Luma need to fund during the first 30, 60, and 90 days?
- Why it matters: Founder-funded services can quietly become more expensive than the equity headline suggests.

### ip_ownership

- Question: Who owns all code, data, models, proof artifacts, brand work, and derivative IP created during the engagement?
- Why it matters: Luma must preserve ownership and clean chain-of-title for future financing, grants, and patents.

### deliverables

- Question: What are the named deliverables in the first 30 days, and what acceptance criteria prove completion?
- Why it matters: Credibility support should become inspectable assets, not vague advice.

### funding_support

- Question: What investor, grant, customer, or partner introductions are committed versus best-efforts?
- Why it matters: Equity should buy leverage, not only service capacity.

### termination

- Question: What happens to equity, work product, data access, and confidentiality if either side stops early?
- Why it matters: Exit terms prevent dead equity and stranded IP.

### conflicts

- Question: Do they work with any overlapping companies or investors that create conflicts?
- Why it matters: The proof stack and patent narrative need protected strategic boundaries.

### safer_start

- Question: Can the relationship start with a lower-risk paid or option sprint before final equity?
- Why it matters: A short credibility sprint can prove fit before major dilution.
