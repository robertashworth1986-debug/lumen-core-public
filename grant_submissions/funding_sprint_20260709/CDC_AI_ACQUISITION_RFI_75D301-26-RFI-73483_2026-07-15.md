## Submission Posture

LumenCore should respond as a prototype-stage AI evidence and evaluation tool for the
federal acquisition lifecycle. The response must state that the product is still in
development and has not been deployed by a federal agency or commercially validated.

The strongest fit is not autonomous acquisition decision-making. It is a bounded layer
that helps acquisition teams register sources and requirements, compare candidate AI
tools against locked baselines, preserve negative results, and produce reviewer-facing
evidence manifests with human approval gates.

## Reviewer-Safe Claims

Use:

- Founder-built prototype-stage platform.
- Hash-based provenance and run manifests.
- Locked evaluation plans and baseline-vs-candidate replay.
- Human approval gates and explicit claim boundaries.
- Public proof gateway and internally reproducible evidence artifacts.
- Available for a controlled, non-production demonstration using public or
  buyer-authorized data.

Do not use:

- CDC-validated, government-approved, production-ready, or deployed.
- Commercially proven or used by federal agencies.
- Certified AI assurance, ATO, CMMC certification, or FedRAMP authorization.
- Guaranteed savings, accuracy, compliance, or acquisition outcomes.
- Autonomous proposal selection or source-selection authority.

## Proposed Demonstration

Demonstrate a synthetic or public-data acquisition workflow in which LumenCore:

1. Registers a requirement, evidence sources, evaluation criteria, and incumbent
   process before scoring.
2. Compares two candidate AI-assisted workflows against the locked baseline.
3. Records provenance, configuration, outputs, exceptions, uncertainty, and reviewer
   decisions in a machine-readable manifest.
4. Produces a concise evidence packet that distinguishes measured results from
   assumptions and blocks unsupported conclusions.
