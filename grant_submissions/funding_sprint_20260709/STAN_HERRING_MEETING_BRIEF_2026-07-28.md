## 45-Second Opening

LumenCore is an evidence and decision workflow for work that is easy to claim
and hard to verify. The strongest near-term product is not the entire research
stack. It is a bounded proof layer that packages an input, protocol, run,
result, limitations, and chain-of-custody into a reviewer-ready capsule.

The public repository demonstrates reproducible software and protocol behavior
and includes bounded internal replay evidence. It does not yet establish
independent validation, production performance, customer savings, or market
adoption. I want your help choosing the first buyer and designing the smallest
external evaluation that can turn the current engineering proof into trusted
commercial evidence.

## Product Wedge

Lead with: **reviewer-ready evidence capsules for regulated or high-consequence
AI, data, and automation workflows**.

Initial buyer hypotheses to test:

- federal or regulated contractors assembling auditable technical evidence;
- engineering teams that must reproduce model, API, or data-pipeline results;
- compliance and assurance teams that need claim-to-evidence traceability;
- pilot sponsors that need a preserved baseline, protocol, result, and
  limitations before authorizing a larger deployment.

Treat energy forecasting, infrastructure monitoring, signal research, and
quant workflows as demonstration domains. Do not present all domains as
finished products.

## What Is Proven

- A public reviewer entry point and public source repository exist.
- The software can produce structured, hash-bound proof artifacts.
- The workflow separates authorization, execution, observation, and claims.
- Bounded internal tests and replay artifacts exist and can be inspected.
- Negative results and unmet gates can be preserved rather than hidden.

## What Is Not Proven

- No independent third party has validated broad performance.
- No production customer outcome or realized savings is established.
- No market segment, pricing model, or repeatable sales motion is established.
- A hash chain proves artifact integrity, not the truth of an underlying claim.
- Internal tests do not establish superiority over an incumbent baseline.

## Bounded External Evaluation Ask

Propose one 2-4 week evaluation with:

- one authorized dataset or workflow;
- one named incumbent baseline;
- metrics and exclusions locked before scoring;
- a frozen environment and reproducible run instructions;
- preserved failures and negative results;
- a reviewer-signed receipt stating exactly what was and was not reproduced;
- no production, savings, or superiority claim unless the protocol supports it.
