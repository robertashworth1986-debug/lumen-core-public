These routes can reduce infrastructure cost or start an investor conversation. None promises cash today, and none should be represented as approved until an official acceptance is received.

## Third Sphere Seedstrap Application

### One-Line Company Description

LumenCore is a proof-to-pilot AI validation platform that helps energy and infrastructure buyers decide whether a forecasting model deserves an independently reviewed pilot.

### Problem

Energy and infrastructure organizations are adopting AI faster than they can verify it. A promising model can look strong in an internal experiment while failing against the buyer's actual baseline, data authority, evaluation window, or operating constraints. Buyers need a reproducible decision layer that preserves both positive and negative results before an AI system reaches operational use.

### Solution

LumenCore takes an approved time-series dataset and locks the baseline, candidate, metrics, and evaluation window before comparison. It runs past-only replays, preserves wins, losses, ties, and abstentions, and issues hash-verifiable evidence receipts. The initial commercial path is a fixed-scope evidence review, followed by a buyer-controlled historical replay pilot. Annual software and evidence-hosting licenses begin only after an independent acceptance gate passes.

### Climate and Planet-Positive Component

The first market wedge is grid, utility, and infrastructure forecasting. LumenCore is designed to help technical teams reject weak forecasting candidates earlier and advance only models that survive buyer-defined evidence gates. The intended climate value is better validation of tools used for grid planning, demand forecasting, renewable integration, and resilient infrastructure. This is an intended use and validation objective, not a current claim of measured emissions reduction, energy savings, or field impact.

### Current Stage and Evidence

LumenCore is pre-revenue and pilot-stage. A working technical MVP and public reviewer surface exist, with locked baseline-versus-candidate protocols, past-only evaluation, explicit abstention, negative-result preservation, reproducible manifests, and hash-verifiable receipts. Internal replay experiments cover public energy and other time-series sources with named baselines. Those results remain internal evidence and are not field validation, independent validation, customer results, or realized savings.

EPRI initiated an Open Power AI onboarding information exchange, a bounded technical package was sent to LANL with no reply observed in the latest full-thread check, and federal capability responses have recorded transmission receipts. These are engagement milestones only; they are not endorsements, partnerships, awards, contracts, or customer traction.

### Customer and Commercial Model

The initial customers are utilities, grid analytics firms, national laboratories, agencies, and industrial operators that own historical time-series data and an accepted incumbent baseline. Current planning assumptions are:

- $5,000-$10,000 for a scoped evidence-review sprint.
- $20,000-$50,000 for a buyer-controlled historical replay pilot.
- $60,000-$150,000 annually for software, evidence hosting, and validation support after independent validation.

These are planning ranges, not booked revenue, approved quotes, or signed customer commitments.

### Six-Month Plan

1. Complete one independent reproduction of a bounded LumenCore evidence package.
2. Convert one qualified technical conversation into a buyer-owned replay pilot with a locked baseline and preregistered acceptance metric.
3. Package the workflow into a stable buyer portal with reviewer-safe access and reproducible receipts.
4. Complete security, legal, and data-authority requirements for a paid evidence review.
5. Build a repeatable founder-led sales process focused on accounts that control relevant data and an economic decision.
6. Use non-dilutive applications and compute-credit programs to extend runway without weakening evidence standards.
