## Proof Ladder

### Level 1 - Provenance

What it proves:

- The artifact existed at a point in time.
- The artifact can be hashed and rechecked.
- The source path and reviewer purpose are known.

What it does not prove:

- Performance, market value, deployment success, or agency acceptance.

Required artifacts:

- SHA-256 manifest.
- Copy receipt.
- Source path and destination path.
- Reviewer-safe README.

### Level 2 - Public Deployment

What it proves:

- Public reviewer surfaces and proof feeds are reachable and hash-aligned at verification time.

What it does not prove:

- Field performance, live operational success, or customer outcomes.

Required artifacts:

- Live domain deployment state.
- Required remote hash match count.
- Public proof gateway link.
- Deployment verification note.

### Level 3 - Source Inventory

What it proves:

- LumenCore can register data sources, mark measured vs unmeasured state, and track source coverage.

What it does not prove:

- That every source is complete, authoritative, or suitable for every domain.

Required artifacts:

- Source inventory summary.
- Measured provider count.
- Row count.
- Timestamp.
- Missing/stale source list.

### Level 4 - Locked Replay

What it proves:

- Candidate methods can be compared against baselines under frozen source, metric, and scenario constraints.

What it does not prove:

- Real-world performance, universal superiority, or realized financial/safety outcomes.

Required artifacts:

- Locked source bundle.
- Baseline registry.
- Candidate registry.
- Run manifest.
- Metric report.
- Negative-result record.

### Level 5 - Domain Translation

What it proves:

- The generic proof engine can be translated into a domain-specific reviewer packet: defense maritime, organizational digital twin, TSMO, data center infrastructure, scientific instrumentation, or AI workflow governance.

What it does not prove:

- Domain authority, final deployment suitability, or certification.

Required artifacts:

- Opportunity-specific capability statement.
- Claim-boundary section.
- Proof gateway link.
- Domain-specific baseline plan.
- External validation ask.

### Level 6 - External Validation Gate

What it proves:

- An external owner accepted or supplied the data, baseline, metric, and evaluation rule.

What it does not prove automatically:

- Commercial scale or broad generalization beyond the accepted test window.

Required artifacts:

- Signed or emailed data/baseline acceptance.
- External-owner metric definition.
- Holdout window.
- Result packet.
- Reviewer signoff or written feedback.
