## Official USPTO Anchors

USPTO provisional application guidance:

- https://www.uspto.gov/patents/basics/apply/provisional-application

Protocol facts:

- A provisional application has a 12-month pendency from filing.
- A corresponding nonprovisional must be filed during that 12-month period to benefit from the provisional filing date.
- A provisional is not examined on the merits.
- The nonprovisional claimed subject matter must be supported by the provisional.
- USPTO recommends consulting a patent practitioner.

USPTO utility patent filing guide:

- https://www.uspto.gov/patents/basics/apply/utility-patent

Protocol facts:

- Provisional applications are automatically abandoned 12 months after filing and are not examined.
- Nonprovisional applications are examined and may issue if patentability requirements are met.

USPTO pro bono source:

- https://www.uspto.gov/subscription-center/2023/connecting-eligible-inventors-and-entrepreneurs-free-legal-assistance

Protocol facts:

- USPTO describes the Patent Pro Bono Program as a nationwide network connecting eligible under-resourced inventors and small businesses with volunteer patent professionals.
