What it actually is

A governed research platform, not a performance promise.

LumenCore organizes source-native benchmarks, reviewer artifacts, and opportunity workflows. It records negative findings and blocks claims that are not supported by the declared baselines, sample gates, and receipts.

Measured-source controls

Source custody, freshness, task compatibility, units, and snapshot hashes are tracked separately from performance evidence.

Source-native benchmark engine

Candidates are evaluated only against baselines and metrics declared for the same task. Exploratory sweeps cannot select a confirmatory holdout winner.

Evidence receipts

SHA-256 manifests bind reviewable artifacts, source snapshots, builders, and tests. A hash proves identity, not model skill.

Human authority boundary

Trading remains paper/replay-only. External sends, submissions, certifications, terms, payments, and deployments require explicit human action.
