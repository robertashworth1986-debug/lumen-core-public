What it actually is

A research platform — not a fund, not a signal service.

LumenCore is the engine and the evidence trail. It validates strategies, logs every decision with cryptographic hashing, and surfaces the truth — including when strategies fail walk-forward. That radical transparency is the brand.

Multi-source data backbone

Kraken USD universe (~650 pairs), Alpaca SIP equities (200 stocks × 2y hourly), Alpaca crypto (24 pairs). Production paid-tier feeds.

Walk-forward truth engine

Rolling train/test windows, 25bps cost-adjusted, parallelized across all CPU cores. In-sample fits get exposed automatically — no cherry-picking.

Hash-chained audit ledger

Every order, signal, and result hashed with SHA-256 and chained. Frozen-delta snapshots for institutional review.

Live execution proven

Real Kraken transactions with verifiable TXIDs (see proof section below). This isn’t a paper-trading screenshot factory.
