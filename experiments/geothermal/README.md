# Utah FORGE geothermal benchmark lane

Source: Utah FORGE Wells 16A(78)-32 and 16B(78)-32 Extended Circulation Test Data — August and September 2024.

Official GDR submission: 1683
DOI: 10.15121/2475065

The source archive contains raw and uncorrected field data, calibration data, and a final report. Measurements include time-stamped wellhead pressure, flow rate, and temperature data recorded at 30-second intervals during the extended circulation test.

Claim boundary: this lane treats the archive as external field evidence. No optimization or superiority claim may be promoted unless a frozen baseline, metric, preprocessing record, and acceptance threshold are declared before scoring.
