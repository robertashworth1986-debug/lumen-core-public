# PowerPoint Visual Direction - Geometric Holographic Hi-Def

## Core aesthetic
- Dark obsidian base with cyan-electric and ember accents.
- Geometric line lattices, radial light blooms, and thin neon separators.
- Strong headline typography with Orbitron-class futuristic faces.
- Use high contrast and negative space to keep readability premium.

## Slide system
- Slide 1: Hero statement with one hard metric and one hard proof line.
- Slide 2: Problem geometry map (fragmented data to unified command).
- Slide 3: Platform architecture (ingestion -> scoring -> control -> evidence).
- Slide 4: Live proof dashboard collage with timestamps.
- Slide 5: Valuation and licensing bands (conservative/base/upside).
- Slide 6: Go-to-market flywheel and conversion math.
- Slide 7: Ask and call scheduling QR.

## Color tokens
- Obsidian: #0B1117
- Graphite: #101A24
- Neon Mint: #00E6C3
- Electric Blue: #32B8FF
- Ember: #FF6A2A
- Platinum Text: #F3F8FF

## Motion intent
- Use restrained reveal: lane-by-lane wipes and staggered metric arrivals.
- Avoid gimmicks. Every animation should explain sequence or causality.

## Output targets
- Master deck: 16:9 presentation
- Print companion: PDF high quality
- Booth loop: auto-advance 7 to 10 second cadence per slide
