## 60-second opening

If there is a fit, I would like to leave with one small non-proprietary next step and the right owner for it.

## LumenCore's narrow contribution

LumenCore should be positioned as an evidence and integration layer, not as a substitute for VISION's protected technology.

The proposed role is:

- package authorized historical or synthetic sensor windows;
- identify the incumbent monitoring or alarm baseline;
- lock the primary metric, threshold, replay window, and failure rules before scoring;
- run candidate and baseline under the same conditions;
- retain missed detections, false alarms, incomplete runs, latency, exceptions, and negative results;
- create SHA-256 input/output manifests and offline verification instructions;
- issue a bounded Proof Capsule and next-pilot decision;
- translate validated technical performance into an operator review and commercialization package without claiming field validation prematurely.

## Proposed 30-day evaluation contract

Offer this only after the PI confirms that the architecture and rights make it sensible.

### Inputs

- one LANL-approved synthetic or non-proprietary sensor dataset, or a data-owner-authorized historical window;
- one named incumbent baseline;
- one VISION output mode suitable for comparison;
- a written data-rights and disclosure boundary.

### Pre-registered rules

- one primary metric and acceptance threshold;
- secondary metrics for false alarms, misses, latency, coverage, and operator review burden as applicable;
- a held-out window or frozen seeds;
- no post-outcome parameter tuning;
- explicit incomplete-run and rejection rules;
- a defined human-review step.

### Deliverables

- source and rights label;
- frozen run contract;
- baseline and candidate outputs;
- input/output SHA-256 manifest;
- negative-result and failure register;
- offline verifier instructions;
- one-page operator summary;
- bounded decision: promote, rerun, external review, hold, or reject.

### Boundary

This would be replay or bench evidence only unless a qualified industrial data owner validates the result under agreed field conditions. It would not establish certification, guaranteed savings, production readiness, customer deployment, or LANL endorsement.

## What Robert should not disclose

Keep the conversation non-proprietary unless a written agreement and an authorized disclosure path are established.

Safe materials are the public proof-to-pilot method, public repository, claim-boundary documents, Proof Capsule schema, and a hypothetical evaluation contract.

## Listen for these signals

### Strong fit

- LANL needs an integration, evaluation, evidence, or operator-reporting layer;
- the PI can provide a non-proprietary test surface;
- the licensing team can identify a clear option or evaluation path;
- a target industrial partner or data owner exists;
- LANL values held-out replay, baseline discipline, provenance, or failure retention.

### Weak fit

- the licensable package already contains a complete validation and commercialization stack;
- VISION requires hardware, facilities, or data that LumenCore cannot currently access;
- there is no permissible non-proprietary evaluation surface;
- the immediate need is a capitalized manufacturer rather than a software/integration partner;
- the available license terms or field constraints do not match LumenCore's intended role.
