# ProofLock Opportunity Sprint — Data Handling Schedule

This schedule is a buyer-review template. It becomes binding only when the buyer
and LumenCore incorporate a completed copy into a signed scope or other
controlling agreement. It is not a claim that LumenCore is certified under a
security or privacy standard.

technically reachable.

## 2. Accepted data

The minimum necessary material may be accepted only when the buyer has the
right to share it and lists it in the signed source register:

- official solicitations, amendments, instructions, and public Q&A;
- buyer-authorized technical evidence, prior submissions, resumes, budgets,
  attachments, and package inventories;
- organization facts supplied or confirmed by an authorized buyer
  representative; and
- derived registers, blocker ledgers, decision records, hashes, and reports
  created within the accepted scope.

Each non-public source must have an owner, rights status, purpose, approved
handler, storage location, and retention/deletion rule before inspection.

## 3. Prohibited by default

Do not send or provide access to any of the following unless a separate written
handling plan and appropriate contract expressly authorize it:

- passwords, API keys, private keys, one-time codes, or other credentials;
- bank, payment-card, tax-account, Social Security, or government-identity
  numbers;
- classified information, Controlled Unclassified Information, export-
  controlled technical data, or material subject to a security agreement;
- protected health information, regulated personal records, biometric data, or
  unnecessary personal data;
- attorney-client privileged communications or attorney work product;
- source code, patent-enabling detail, or trade secrets outside the named
  review scope; and
- third-party confidential material the buyer is not authorized to disclose.

If prohibited or out-of-scope material is encountered, processing stops for
that item. The material is quarantined or returned as the signed schedule
requires; it is not silently added to an evidence bundle.

## 4. External AI and service-provider rule

Client-confidential content must not be submitted to an external AI, model,
search, storage, analytics, or automation provider unless that provider, the
specific purpose, the allowed data classes, and the buyer's written approval
are recorded above. Authorization to use one provider does not authorize any
other provider or broader use.

Public-source research may use ordinary public research tools. Public access
does not establish that a source is accurate, controlling, current, or safe to
promote; the source register and claim boundary still govern the result.

## 5. Access, storage, and transfer controls

- Access is limited to the named operator(s) and buyer-authorized reviewers.
- Client-confidential material is kept outside the public repository and public
  website.
- Files are stored only in the approved locations and transferred only through
  the approved channels.
- Working copies, exports, screenshots, and generated artifacts inherit the
  source's handling restriction unless the buyer approves a narrower public
  derivative in writing.
- A hash proves byte identity for a named artifact; it does not prove
  confidentiality, accuracy, ownership, deletion, or authority.

## 7. Incident and exception handling

A handling incident includes suspected unauthorized access or disclosure,
credential exposure, use of an unapproved provider, loss of a controlled copy,
or discovery of prohibited data. On discovery, LumenCore will:

1. stop the affected processing;
2. preserve a minimal incident record without expanding disclosure;
3. notify the buyer through the agreed incident channel within `[TIME WINDOW]`;
4. identify the known data class, affected system, containment step, and
   unresolved facts; and
5. resume only after the decision owner approves a written recovery step.

This schedule does not promise a forensic conclusion, regulatory determination,
or legal notification decision. Those require the responsible owner and, where
appropriate, qualified counsel or security professionals.

## 8. Publicity and evidence boundary

No buyer name, logo, confidential source, result, testimonial, customer claim,
partnership claim, validation claim, or case study may be published without
written permission that names the exact approved content. A private review or
paid sprint does not by itself establish endorsement, external validation,
award likelihood, field performance, savings, or permission to identify the
buyer.

## 9. Handoff and acceptance

The final handoff identifies:

- the sources and rights statuses actually used;
- any approved external services actually used;
- the delivered artifacts and their hashes where applicable;
- unresolved facts, incidents, deviations, and exclusions;
- each retained, returned, quarantined, or deleted data class; and
- the person responsible for every remaining human, legal, portal, or payment
  action.

Buyer acceptance of a handoff does not convert a bounded decision record into
certification, legal advice, agency acceptance, eligibility, award, or external
validation.
