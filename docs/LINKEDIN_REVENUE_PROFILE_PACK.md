## 2. About section

> I help technical decision owners determine whether a promising AI, forecast,
> routing, optimization, or infrastructure candidate should move forward—or be
> rerun, held, rejected, or sent to external review.
>
> LumenCore runs a bounded validation sprint around one buyer-owned decision.
> Before scoring, we lock the authorized source, accepted baseline, primary
> metric, held-out window, failure rules, claim boundary, and decision owner.
> Candidate and baseline are then evaluated under the same contract. Positive,
> neutral, incomplete, adverse, and failed findings remain visible.
>
> The result is a reviewer-readable evidence pack: a pre-registered evaluation
> contract, matched results, failure register, SHA-256 custody manifest,
> offline verification instructions, Proof Capsule, and one bounded decision:
> promote, rerun, external review, hold, or reject.
>
> Best first fits: infrastructure and utility forecasting, AI/workflow
> reliability, routing or scheduling comparisons, technical diligence, and
> evidence review for teams that already have an incumbent baseline and a real
> decision deadline.
>
> LumenCore has an active UEI and CAGE code for appropriate vendor-onboarding,
> market-research, subcontracting, and procurement conversations. The standard
> commercial sprint is non-production and does not accept CUI, PHI,
> export-controlled data, credentials, or autonomous-control access without a
> separately authorized path.
>
> The paid entry engagement is a 30-day Launch Replay: one authorized source,
> one accepted baseline, one primary metric, one held-out window, one Proof
> Capsule, and one decision briefing. A favorable outcome, ROI, certification,
> deployment, or field validation is not promised.
>
> If you own a decision that needs defensible evidence, message me with the
> candidate, baseline, metric, and useful-by date. Please do not send datasets,
> credentials, or confidential information in LinkedIn messages.

## 5. First three posts

### Post 1 — buyer problem

> A technical demo is not a decision packet.
>
> When a team says “our model is better,” the next questions are predictable:
> Better than what baseline? On which authorized data? Under what metric? Was
> the holdout fixed before results were visible? What failed?
>
> LumenCore’s 30-day Buyer-Owned Baseline Validation Sprint turns one candidate
> versus one accepted baseline into a bounded, inspectable decision. We lock
> the rules before scoring and keep negative results in the record.
>
> The output is not hype. It is promote, rerun, external review, hold, or
> reject—with manifests, verification instructions, and explicit limits.
>
> If your team has a candidate, an incumbent baseline, and a decision deadline,
> message me the non-confidential version of the decision. Do not send data or
> credentials in DM.

### Post 2 — credibility without overclaiming

> Evidence custody is not authority.
>
> A hash can show whether an artifact changed. It cannot prove field
> performance, ROI, safety, independent validation, or customer acceptance.
>
> That distinction is exactly why LumenCore keeps source rights, baseline,
> metric, holdout, failure rules, evidence state, and claim boundaries separate.
> A result can be useful without being inflated.
>
> I built a public ProofLock demonstration that verifies receipts and refuses to
> turn self-authored evidence into approval. It is a bounded demonstration, not
> a certification claim: <https://lumen-core.ai/build_week/prooflock_console/>

### Post 3 — federal-market signal

> Small businesses are often ready to help before they are easy for a buyer to

>
> LumenCore has an active UEI and CAGE code for appropriate vendor-onboarding,

>

>
