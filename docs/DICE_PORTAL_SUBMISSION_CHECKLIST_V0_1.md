## 3. Technical-volume conformity gate

Before conversion to the official submission format:

- ☐ Abstract ID and project title match the BAAT workspace.
- ☐ Technical areas are selected exactly as permitted by the solicitation.
- ☐ Executive summary states the research hypothesis, not a product guarantee.
- ☐ Heilmeier answers are direct, quantified, and consistent with the work plan.
- ☐ Existing evidence is separated from proposed Phase I/II/III work.
- ☐ Synthetic executors are not described as heterogeneous LLM-agent validation.
- ☐ Kraken/EIA replays are described as deterministic stress signals, not mission data.
- ☐ Mission-success superiority is not claimed where confidence intervals crossed zero.
- ☐ False-rejection increase is retained as a material negative result.
- ☐ Corrected benchmark defect is disclosed in the evidence history.
- ☐ Every numeric claim maps to a named source artifact and manifest status.
- ☐ TA3 compatibility is framed as proposed adaptor/integration work unless tested.
- ☐ Work breakdown, milestones, metrics, and go/no-go criteria use consistent names.
- ☐ Risk register includes technical, integration, scale, security, schedule, cost, and transition risks.
- ☐ Safety boundary excludes weapons control, physical actuation, live production access, autonomous trading, and unauthorized CUI/classified handling.
- ☐ Team/facilities statements contain no invented personnel, affiliations, clearances, access, or equipment.
- ☐ References, figures, captions, equations, acronyms, and cross-references survive PDF conversion.
- ☐ Font, margins, page limits, allowed appendices, and excluded-page rules are verified from the official template.

### Technical stop-ship conditions

Any one of the following blocks final assembly:

1. A claim lacks a named evidence source.
2. A modeled/synthetic result is described as operational proof.
3. A partner or evaluator is named without documented permission.
4. A metric definition changed after results were inspected without a new experiment version.
5. The proposal claims TA3 integration, heterogeneous-agent validation, independent reproduction, or field performance that has not occurred.
6. The PDF exceeds the official page limit or violates template formatting.

## 4. Cost-volume and budget gate

No cost entry should be treated as final until the cost workbook and budget narrative reconcile line by line.

- ☐ Award instrument assumptions are identified as assumptions until confirmed.
- ☐ Period of performance and phase structure match the solicitation.
- ☐ Every task has named labor categories, hours, rates, and calculation basis.
- ☐ Founder labor treatment is documented and reasonable.
- ☐ Consultant/subcontractor scopes, rates, periods, and written quotes are retained.
- ☐ Cloud/HPC/API estimates state units, volumes, rates, and contingency assumptions.
- ☐ Software, data, travel, equipment, materials, and other direct costs have a task nexus.
- ☐ Indirect rates are supported, provisional, or explicitly identified as pending.
- ☐ Fee/profit treatment follows the anticipated instrument and official instructions.
- ☐ Cost-sharing is not claimed unless intentional, allowable, documented, and approved.
- ☐ No unpaid labor, donated access, partner contribution, or matching funds are represented without evidence.
- ☐ Total by task, phase, year, and category reconciles across every document.
- ☐ Budget figures do not exceed the solicitation ceiling.
- ☐ Narrative explains why each major cost is necessary, reasonable, and allocable.
- ☐ Personally identifiable banking or tax information is excluded from drafting repositories.

### Budget stop-ship conditions

- Missing labor rates or hours.
- Unsupported subcontractor/consultant costs.
- Cost workbook and narrative totals differ.
- Incorrect phase lengths or start/end assumptions.
- Any certification concerning accounting systems, indirect rates, allowability, or financial capacity has not been fact-checked and approved.
