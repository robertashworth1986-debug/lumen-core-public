# LumenCore Claim Boundary Register

**Purpose:** Keep public, grant, investor, mentor, and pilot language disciplined.

The rule is simple: every claim must map to evidence and every evidence type must state what it does not prove.

---

## 1. Claim categories

| Category | Meaning | Public use |
|---|---|---|
| Measured | Rows, files, commits, outputs, logs, or runs exist | strongest internal evidence |
| Replay | Controlled replay result exists | useful, not field validation |
| Synthetic | Generated/simulated benchmark result exists | useful for feasibility, not real-world deployment proof |
| Modeled | Simulation or internal modeling output exists | useful with limitations |
| Estimated | Economic conversion or opportunity framing | not revenue, not savings proof |
| Conceptual | Architecture, invention disclosure, roadmap, or theory | not performance proof |
| Externally validated | Qualified outside party or buyer verifies agreed run | strongest external evidence |

---

## 2. Safe master claim

> LumenCore is a founder-built proof-to-pilot architecture that packages measured, replay, synthetic, modeled, and estimated evidence into reviewer-readable reports with explicit source, baseline, locked metric, manifest, result, and claim boundary.

---

## 3. Claims to avoid unless externally supported

Do not claim:

- audited revenue,
- GAAP revenue,
- signed enterprise contracts,
- certified aircraft capability,
- certified suit capability,
- weapons capability,
- autonomous physical control,
- medical diagnosis,
- field-validated savings,
- guaranteed ROI,
- agency endorsement,
- grant award likelihood,
- certified infrastructure control,
- CMMC certification,
- field-ready DoD deployment,
- operational harbor/Navy/SSDS performance,
- production customer savings,
- live autonomous revenue.

---

## 4. Safer replacements

| Risky phrase | Safer replacement |
|---|---|
| production-grade institutional intelligence platform | founder-built proof-to-pilot validation architecture |
| real money / real TXIDs / verified profit | runtime/evidence surfaces exist; trading profit is not claimed unless externally reconciled |
| live autonomous trading system | human-reviewed runtime-control and evidence workflow |
| DoD adaptive systems | defense-relevant controlled simulation/replay concepts |
| certified aircraft capability | simulation-first LumaJet proof lane |
| haptic suit capability | non-actuating LumaSuit/LumaSkin digital-twin concept |
| field-proven ROI | internal modeled or replay evidence pending external validation |
| guaranteed savings | estimated opportunity value pending buyer-authorized validation |
| partner/customer/pilot confirmed | outreach, review, or proposed pilot unless signed documentation exists |
| agency validation | agency-aligned proposal/review lane unless written agency confirmation exists |

---

## 5. Required proof language

Every public proof artifact should include:

- evidence type,
- source/dataset,
- baseline/comparator,
- locked metric,
- run window,
- manifest/hash status,
- limitation,
- what is not proven,
- next validation gate.

## 6. Luma module-specific boundaries

### LumaTrader
Safe: market-data ingestion, scoring, dashboards, evidence discipline, runtime controls, paper/shadow evaluation where supported.  
Avoid: audited profit, guaranteed trading performance, live autonomous revenue.

### LumaScout
Safe: discovery/scouting intelligence branch and API/dashboard concept.  
Avoid: relying on live service continuity until billing/API status is current.

### LumaJet
Safe: synthetic/simulation geometry, routing, thermal, diagnostic, resilience, and baseline evidence.  
Avoid: aircraft certification, flight capability, propulsion build instructions, autonomous physical control.

### LumaSuit / LumaSkin
Safe: non-actuating smart-skin, haptics, human-performance digital twin, fatigue/thermal/strain scoring, bench sensors, mentor/lab review.  
Avoid: powered armor, weapons, medical diagnosis, flight control, autonomous actuation, certified safety.

### EchoForm / Identity Architecture
Safe: identity-consistent digital-twin architecture, bio-digital signature concepts, simulation, interface design, proof packaging.  
Avoid: medical, biometric certification, or safety-critical identity claims without qualified validation.

### FlowForm / LumaSpiral / EtherFrame / AetherReach / LumenShell
Safe: architecture, geometry-informed routing, simulation, proof packaging, interface concepts, patentable system design.  
Avoid: physics-defying performance claims, certified field control, or claims of external validation without proof.

### FlowForm curved electronics / honeycomb energy / ground robotics
Safe: integrity-bound concept renders, null hypotheses, matched-test requirements, conventional simulation plans, non-energized or low-energy lab planning, and qualified-review requests.
Avoid: describing renders as CAD, schematics, prototypes, or test results; do not claim signal-integrity, EMI, thermal, battery, propagation, autonomy, payload, safety, reliability, manufacturing, certification, or deployment performance.

### LumenFrame / STL
Safe: one founder-originated V1 digital mesh exists. Its receipt supports exact claims of 4,884 bytes, binary STL format, 96 triangles, 64 unique vertices, 144 unique edges, eight disconnected closed components, zero degenerate triangles, zero boundary or non-manifold edges, and extents of 60 × 87.5 × 120 in undeclared source units.
Avoid: print success, structural suitability, dimensional accuracy, aerospace use, manufacturability, or performance. Empty V3 STL stubs and placeholder photo files are not evidence.

### Smart City / Telecom
Safe: read-only requirements, deterministic simulation, shadow-mode telemetry, matched RF/thermal coupon planning, accepted incumbent baseline, locked metric, and human-owned decision.
Avoid: city or network control, autonomous mobility, commercial-board or 5G replacement, latency, resilience, RF/EMC, privacy compliance, low-EMF safety, savings, production readiness, or deployment.

### Energy / Nuclear Harmonization
Safe: conventional energy-data forecasting, calibration, evidence quality, and historical research-lineage disclosure.
Avoid: nuclear conversion, radiation attenuation, healing, biometric energy transfer, field stabilization, usable-energy generation, safety, facility control, or validated mechanism. The legacy Nuclear Harmonization source remains a restricted speculative archive.

### Dungeon / Dungieon
Safe: state only that the source identity is unresolved.
Avoid: inventing its function, maturity, visual, application, evidence, or relationship to another module.

## 7. Promotion gate

A result can be promoted from internal to public only when this checklist is complete:

- [ ] result has source/dataset named,
- [ ] baseline/comparator named,
- [ ] metric locked before scoring,
- [ ] run type labeled,
- [ ] manifest/hash recorded,
- [ ] limitations written,
- [ ] no forbidden claims present,
- [ ] reviewer-safe summary prepared,
- [ ] founder approval given.

---

## 8. Bottom line

Evidence first. Boundaries always. Founder ownership preserved.
