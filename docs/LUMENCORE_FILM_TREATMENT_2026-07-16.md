# LumenCore Film Treatment

## Working title

**THE STOP: When the Stronger Test Says No**

## Logline

A relentless founder and an AI collaborator search for measurable advantage, then discover that their most credible result is not a spectacular win but a system disciplined enough to reject its own promising signal, preserve the failure, and invite an outsider to reproduce it.

## Why this story works

The central conflict is understandable without selling a scientific fantasy: a diagnostic result looks positive, the stricter reviewer protocol overturns it, and the system blocks economic action. The audience gets a human question with real stakes: can an AI earn trust by recording the moment it should stop?

## Three-act spine

### Act I: Build

- Open on the founder's repeated demand for stronger tests and truthful receipts.
- Move through notebooks, code, equations, dashboards, physical-design artifacts, and the accumulated proof archive.
- Establish the operating promise: no claim outruns its evidence.

### Act II: Gauntlet

- Show the attractive diagnostic result: `+1.446923%` against one global baseline.
- Reveal the stronger reviewer comparison: `-0.287158%` against each pair's strongest named baseline.
- Walk through the five failed promotion checks, `11/20` positive pairs versus the required `12/20`, and `0/20` individually promoted pairs.
- Make the rejection the turning point: execution and capital at risk remain disabled.

### Act III: Invitation

- Preserve the null/adverse result instead of hiding it.
- Hand a blind reproduction kit to an independent reviewer.
- End before validation is claimed. The open question is the invitation: will an outsider reproduce the receipt and try to break the protocol?

## Available screen story

- The existing 32-second **Truth Over Hype** cut is the teaser and visual proof of concept.
- Mission Control, reviewer dashboards, test receipts, checksum chains, and the clean GitHub runner provide the technical world.
- Three unique user video sources exist. The long recording and two controller-board Live Photos are historical physical context only; none is currently receipt-linked as LumenCore performance evidence.
- Conversation motifs available for public-safe captions: "We only claim facts," "I love integrity checks," and "Can we do an independent result receipt yet?"

## Claim boundary

- Source-authentic Kraken data is not Kraken endorsement.
- The diagnostic gain is not a promoted performance claim.
- The stricter gate failed and no economic action is authorized.
- The blind reproduction kit is ready, but no independent execution receipt has been received.
- Physical images and videos are not evidence of built, tested, field-validated, or agency-accepted LumenCore hardware unless a separate receipt establishes that status.
