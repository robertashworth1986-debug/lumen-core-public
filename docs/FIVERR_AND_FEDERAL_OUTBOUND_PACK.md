### Gig description

Your team has a promising AI, forecast, routing, optimization, or
infrastructure candidate. The hard part is deciding whether it actually beats
the accepted alternative under rules fixed before scoring.

I run a bounded validation sprint for **one buyer-owned decision**. We lock the
authorized source, baseline, primary metric, held-out window, failure rules,
and decision owner before the scored run. You receive matched results,
preserved negative findings, SHA-256 manifests, offline verification
instructions, a Proof Capsule, and one bounded decision: promote, rerun,
external review, hold, or reject.

This engagement is appropriate for a startup, lab, operator, investor, or
technical team with one non-production decision to evaluate. It does not
promise favorable performance, ROI, certification, production readiness, or
field validation.

**Before ordering:** send only a non-confidential description of your decision,
candidate, accepted baseline, primary metric, data-rights status, decision
owner, and useful-by date. Do not send datasets, credentials, customer data,
CUI, PHI, payment-card data, export-controlled information, or production
