# LumenCore Strategic Transaction Brief

**Status:** exploratory founder-authorized inquiry; no binding sale, license, financing, or partnership is authorized by this document.

## What LumenCore is

LumenCore is a proof-to-pilot AI validation architecture for organizations that need to determine whether an AI, analytics, or engineering claim is reproducible, properly bounded, and ready for a controlled pilot. The public repository emphasizes artifact custody, deterministic replay, machine-readable evidence states, fail-closed promotion gates, and reviewer handoff packages.

## What is available for discussion

A qualified counterparty may discuss one of five structures:

1. whole-company acquisition;
2. exclusive field-of-use license;
3. non-exclusive technology license;
4. acquihire plus defined IP license;
5. paid validation pilot with a negotiated option to license or acquire.

No structure transfers ownership, rights, control, source code, confidential materials, patent rights, credentials, or data before a signed definitive agreement and completion of the applicable diligence and payment conditions.

## What remains private until qualified diligence

- private financial records and founder personal information;
- unpublished enabling details and patent-sensitive material;
- credentials, tokens, private infrastructure, and private datasets;
- private proposal files and regulated or export-controlled information;
- legal opinions, chain-of-title documents, and transaction drafts;
- buyer-specific pricing, warranties, indemnities, and transition terms.

## Required diligence before any transaction

A serious process should include, as applicable:

- founder identity, authority, and chain-of-title review;
- repository and asset inventory reconciliation;
- patent-record and IP-scope review by qualified counsel;
- financial, tax, liability, and contract diligence;
- security and credential-separation review;
- third-party license, data-rights, and open-source review;
- independent technical execution of the agreed reviewer package;
- buyer-specific integration, support, and transition planning;
- signed definitive documents and verified consideration.
