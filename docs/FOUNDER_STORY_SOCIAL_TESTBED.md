# Founder Story Social Testbed

**Purpose:** Convert authentic founder footage into a reproducible short-form content system without exaggerating technical claims.

## Creative identity

The visual world is a **grandfathered workshop still standing**:

- old wood, repaired surfaces, exposed tools, wires, notebooks, screens,
- natural light mixed with monitor glow,
- visible wear rather than fake luxury,
- modern architecture being built inside imperfect conditions.

The contrast is the story: the environment may look temporary, but the system keeps evolving.

## Character frame

Robert is presented as:

- founder-builder,
- systems architect,
- relentless experimenter,
- direct and imperfect on camera,
- willing to show failed runs and repairs,
- disciplined about separating ideas from proven results.

Avoid portraying him as superhuman, divinely certified, or already externally validated.

## Core narrative

```text
I did not wait for perfect conditions.
I built a proof system inside the conditions I had.
Every idea gets a baseline.
Every run gets a lineage.
Every claim gets a boundary.
Bounded light speed.
```

## First footage-selection pass

Tag every usable shot with:

- `shot_id`
- timestamp in/out
- action: coding, drawing, repairing, explaining, testing, walking, reflecting
- emotional tone
- visual quality
- audible quote
- technical claim risk
- best platform
- suggested hook

Priority shots:

1. hands physically working,
2. screens showing code or tests,
3. diagrams and notebooks,
4. visible setbacks or repairs,
5. quiet concentration,
6. one direct-to-camera explanation,
7. a concrete result or test output.

## Pilot short: "Still Standing"

Target length: 35–50 seconds.

### Sequence

1. **0–3 s — Hook**
   Exterior or rough workspace detail.
   On-screen text: `THE SHOP LOOKED TEMPORARY.`

2. **3–10 s — Human proof**
   Hands, wiring, typing, sketches.
   Voice: `I didn't wait for perfect conditions.`

3. **10–20 s — Architecture**
   Code, lineage chart, experiment output.
   Voice: `I built a system where every idea gets a baseline and every result has ancestry.`

4. **20–32 s — New lexicon**
   Alpha Echo -> iSeed -> Bounded Infinity animation.
   Voice: `The first signal is Alpha Echo. The reproducible kernel is iSeed. The evolution stays open—but bounded.`

5. **32–43 s — Proof boundary**
   Testing footage or failed run.
   Voice: `I don't call it proven until the evidence survives the test.`

6. **43–50 s — Signature**
   Founder looking at screen or leaving workspace.
   Text: `BOUNDLESS SEARCH. BOUNDED PROOF.`
   End mark: `LumenCore | Bounded Light Speed`

## Content experiments

Run three hook variants against the same body:

- A: `I built this before I had the room to build it.`
- B: `The shack was rickety. The architecture wasn't.`
- C: `What happens when every idea has to prove its ancestry?`

Locked metrics:

- 3-second hold rate,
- average percentage watched,
- rewatch rate,
- saves,
- qualified profile visits,
- comments asking about the technology,
- inbound pilot or reviewer interest.

Do not optimize on views alone.

## Platform exports

- TikTok / Reels / Shorts: 9:16, captions burned in, 20–60 seconds.
- LinkedIn: 4:5 or 1:1, slower pacing, stronger proof language.
- YouTube founder log: 16:9, 4–8 minutes, full context and limitations.

## Evidence and safety rules

- Never show credentials, private emails, API keys, personal addresses, or unpublished patent detail.
- Blur screens when necessary.
- Separate synthetic, modeled, replay, measured, and externally validated results.
- Do not claim government partnership, deployment, certification, savings, or revenue without records.
- Do not romanticize unsafe driving, exhaustion, or dangerous equipment use.

## Reproducible production package

Each published clip receives:

- source-file hash,
- shot-list JSON,
- transcript,
- edit-decision list,
- caption file,
- music/license record,
- export settings,
- platform/date/version,
- performance results,
- next-test decision.

This turns social media into another LumenCore evidence lane rather than random posting.
