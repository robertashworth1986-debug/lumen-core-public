# EVTit / Black Dog Call Agenda — LumenCore

**Goal:** Convert the Terry / Bruno / Aron conversation into a founder-protected review, pilot-routing, or scoped-services path — not an outside ownership path.

---

## 1. Opening summary

LumenCore is a founder-owned proof-to-pilot AI infrastructure validation platform.

It helps buyers test AI/infrastructure claims against accepted baselines through controlled replay, locked metrics, evidence manifests, and buyer-readable proof reports.

The platform already has a public repo trail, proof-to-pilot documents, live-breadth evidence discipline, LumaJet/LumaSuit/LumaTrader/LumaScout module branches, and a patent/architecture spine. The next step is clean packaging and external validation routing, not giving away the platform.

---

## 2. What LumenCore may need from EVTit

The useful role is focused and founder-protective:

> Review the proof stack, help route pilots or investors, and optionally support scoped UI/report/productization work under terms that preserve all pre-existing LumenCore IP.

Potential scoped support:

- product architecture review,
- web portal cleanup,
- replay/synthetic runner productization,
- evidence manifest automation,
- customer proof report template,
- pilot intake flow,
- API cost/reliability controls,
- investor/federal demo packet support.

---

## 3. Questions for EVTit

1. Does LumenCore fit initial technical review or pilot-routing support?
2. What would EVTit need to verify the proof-to-pilot workflow?
3. What scoped 30-day deliverable would support pilots without touching founder-owned core IP?
4. What build scope is too large and should be deferred?
5. What terms protect pre-existing founder IP, architecture, names, lexicon, and proof materials?
6. What equity/cash/in-kind model is realistic, if any?
7. Who from EVTit should review the AI/proof-stack materials?
8. What should LumenCore send next, and what should remain private until written terms exist?

Send or present only if requested:

- founder-protected EVTit build scope,
- current proof status,
- founder/IP boundary,

---

## 5. Claim boundaries for the call

Use this exact boundary:

> LumenCore is not claiming certified control, guaranteed savings, certified aircraft or suit capability, weapons capability, autonomous physical control, audited revenue, or field-validated ROI. The claim is that LumenCore can build auditable replay-validation packages that allow a buyer to test a proposed AI/infrastructure method against their accepted baseline under a pre-registered metric.

---

## 6. Founder/IP boundary for the call

Use this exact boundary:

> Reviewing, advising, or proposing services does not grant rights to pre-existing LumenCore IP. LumenCore owns the platform, architecture, names, modules, lexicon, prior code, proof stack, and patentable structure unless a written founder-protective agreement says otherwise.

---

## 7. Desired outcome

Best outcome:

- EVTit agrees LumenCore fits initial technical review or pilot-routing support,
- EVTit requests the founder-protected review packet,
- a follow-up technical session is scheduled with Bruno/Aron,
- any proposed build scope is narrow, written, and founder-IP protected,
