## 1. Current posture

LumenCore is best presented as a **founder-owned proof-to-pilot AI infrastructure validation architecture**.

The strongest public claim is not that LumenCore has certified field performance, audited savings, or autonomous control. The strongest claim is that LumenCore can organize complex evidence into reviewer-readable Proof Capsules:

- source or dataset,
- incumbent baseline or comparator,
- metric locked before scoring,
- controlled replay, synthetic, bench, or measured run,
- SHA/hash manifest,
- result summary,
- limitation and claim boundary,
- next pilot decision.

## 4. Proof Capsule gate

No result should be promoted as a finding unless it has:

1. source/dataset named,
2. baseline/comparator named,
3. metric locked before scoring,
4. run type labeled as measured, replay, synthetic, modeled, or estimated,
5. manifest/hash attached or queued,
6. result summarized,
7. failure notes included,
8. claim boundary included,
9. next pilot decision stated.

---

## 5. External validation path

External validation requires a buyer, lab, mentor, or qualified reviewer to define or approve:

- data rights,
- baseline,
- metric,
- replay/simulation window,
- acceptance threshold,
- reporting format,
- allowed claim after review.

Until that happens, internal evidence remains internal evidence.

The next product step is not inventing proof; it is promoting current evidence into clean, bounded, reviewer-safe Proof Capsules that support pilots, grants, mentors, and investors without giving away ownership.
