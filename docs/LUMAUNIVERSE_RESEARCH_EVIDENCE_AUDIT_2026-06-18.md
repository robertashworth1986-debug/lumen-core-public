# LumaUniverse Research Evidence Audit - June 18, 2026

## Decision

The geometry and bio-inspired ideas belong in the research program, but they
must be separated into two lanes:

1. **Findings:** implemented, reproducible comparisons with frozen inputs,
   baselines, outputs, hashes, and explicit limitations.
2. **Candidate families:** falsifiable hypotheses that have not yet completed
   that protocol.

Calling an unimplemented family a finding would weaken a federal proposal.
Negative and null results remain findings when the test design is valid.

## Promotion Protocol

A candidate becomes a research finding only after all of these are present:

1. A versioned mathematical or algorithmic definition with no metaphor-only
   parameters.
2. A preregistered task, baseline set, metric, evaluation budget, and failure
   criterion.
3. Identical train/test splits, compute budgets, and tuning access for every
   family.
4. At least five chronological or scenario folds with seeds and raw inputs
   frozen before scoring.
5. Paired uncertainty intervals, null or surrogate tests where appropriate,
   and multiple-comparison control across the full family search.
6. Runtime, memory, latency, and robustness measurements, not accuracy alone.
7. A manifest containing SHA-256 hashes, environment versions, command line,
   source commit, and supersession status.
8. Negative, null, and failed runs retained in the evidence ledger.

## Federal Proposal Use

The defensible preliminary finding is not that natural geometry always wins.
It is that LumenCore now has an evidence-gated architecture capable of testing
many candidate model families, abstaining when they fail, and preserving an
auditable trail. The bio-inspired candidates can appear as Phase I ablations or
work packages. They should not appear in an abstract, technical objective, or
commercial claim as demonstrated advantages until they pass the promotion
protocol.

For HarborSentinel, the most relevant future tests are mycelial resilient
routing, bird consensus, and hibernation-style bounded wake logic. For DICE,
the most relevant tests are mycelial trust propagation and swarm coordination
under independent compromise, collusion, monitor shift, and constrained
communications. Trading results should remain outside those mission claims.
