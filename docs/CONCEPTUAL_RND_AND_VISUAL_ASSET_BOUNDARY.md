# Conceptual R&D and Visual Asset Boundary

## Purpose

LumenCore uses diagrams, geometric studies, system maps, concept renders, and speculative visualizations to communicate research directions. Those assets can be useful design records without being treated as experimental evidence, validated engineering, deployed capability, patent scope, or commercial proof.

This document defines the public claim boundary for those assets.

## Covered asset classes

This boundary applies to visual or narrative material involving, for example:

- spiral, helix, gyroid, phyllotaxis, manifold, graph, or non-Euclidean geometries;
- cymatic, harmonic, resonance, phase-locking, frequency, or field-shaping concepts;
- particle, ion, atomic, electromagnetic, thermal, fluid, neural, or molecular illustrations;
- curved-board, battery, antenna, cooling, flowform, suit, vehicle, aerospace, medical, energy, defense, or infrastructure concepts;
- white-hole, echo-form, hollow-twin, digital-twin, and other founder-originated system metaphors;
- AI-generated or manually composed diagrams, renders, posters, and pitch graphics.

## Evidence-state ladder

A visual asset must use exactly one of the following states unless a stronger state is supported by a separately linked record.

| State | What exists | Permitted description |
|---|---|---|
| **CONCEPT ILLUSTRATION** | A visual idea, metaphor, or design sketch | “Concept illustration,” “design direction,” or “research visualization” |
| **DEFINED MATHEMATICAL OBJECT** | Equations, coordinates, topology, units, and constraints are stated | “Defined geometry/model for evaluation” |
| **COMPUTATIONAL MODEL** | Executable model and named assumptions exist | “Computational model” or “simulation candidate” |
| **FIRST-PARTY SIMULATION** | The author ran the named model from pinned inputs and preserved outputs | “First-party simulated result for the named configuration” |
| **INDEPENDENTLY REPRODUCED** | A qualified non-author reran the frozen package and returned a receipt | “Independently reproduced under the named protocol” |
| **EXPERIMENTALLY TESTED** | Physical apparatus, calibration, controls, uncertainty, and results are documented | “Experimentally tested under the named conditions” |
| **FIELD VALIDATED** | A data owner or operating partner validated the result in an agreed environment | “Field validated for the named use case and conditions” |

No lower state may be described using the language of a higher state.

## Current public boundary

Unless a linked Proof Capsule or external record says otherwise, LumenCore concept art is classified as **CONCEPT ILLUSTRATION**.

A concept image does not by itself establish:

- physical feasibility;
- numerical performance;
- safety, certification, or production readiness;
- experimental or field validation;
- universal geometric, harmonic, routing, thermal, electromagnetic, or AI superiority;
- agency, laboratory, consortium, customer, investor, or attorney endorsement;
- issued, granted, enforceable, or attorney-approved patent rights;
- realized savings, revenue, valuation, contract value, or customer adoption.

## Required asset label

Every public conceptual image or diagram should have an adjacent caption or metadata note substantially equivalent to:

> **Conceptual R&D visualization — not experimental, field, safety, certification, patent-scope, performance, or deployment evidence.**

When an asset reflects a defined model or simulation, the caption must also identify the exact repository path or Proof Capsule that supports the higher state.

## Promotion requirements

Before promoting an asset above **CONCEPT ILLUSTRATION**, record:

1. the exact mathematical or engineering object being evaluated;
2. units, coordinate system or chart, boundary and initial conditions, and material assumptions;
3. accepted baseline or comparator selected before scoring;
4. metric, threshold, uncertainty method, and failure rules locked before execution;
5. source rights, code commit, dependencies, seeds, and artifact hashes;
6. positive, neutral, incomplete, and negative results;
7. known limitations and what the result does not prove;
8. reviewer identity and independence when an external state is claimed;
9. the next decision: promote, rerun, external review, hold, or reject.

## Public wording rules

### Permitted before external evidence

- “LumenCore is exploring…”
- “This visual represents a proposed…”
- “The geometry is a candidate for bounded comparison…”
- “The first-party simulation indicates…under the named assumptions…”
- “External reproduction or physical testing remains required.”

### Prohibited without separately linked evidence

- “proven,” “validated,” “certified,” “production-ready,” or “field-tested”;
- “patented” when only an application, provisional filing, draft, or counsel discussion exists;
- “guaranteed,” “best,” “optimal,” “revolutionary,” “universal,” or “most efficient”;
- quantified savings, reliability, safety, energy, trading, infrastructure, or performance claims inferred only from a concept image;
- language implying that a Government agency, laboratory, consortium, university, customer, investor, or attorney approved the technical conclusion.

## Repository placement

Public conceptual assets should be stored only in a clearly labeled research or concept path, with an adjacent source note containing:

- title and asset identifier;
- creator and generation method;
- creation date;
- concept state;
- source inputs and licensing status;
- whether AI generation was used;
- linked mathematical definition, model, simulation, or Proof Capsule when applicable;
- exact claim boundary.

Concept files must not be placed inside canonical evidence, external-validation, field-validation, customer-proof, award, or production-deployment directories unless the file is supporting context and is explicitly labeled non-evidentiary.

## Operating rule

**Use visuals to explain the research direction. Use Proof Capsules and external records to support claims.**

Bounded light speed means moving quickly without allowing a compelling image to outrun evidence, rights, safety, or human authority.
