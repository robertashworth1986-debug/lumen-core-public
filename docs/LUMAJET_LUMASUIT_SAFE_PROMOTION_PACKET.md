# LumaJet / LumaSuit Safe Promotion Packet

**Purpose:** Promote LumaJet and LumaSuit/LumaSkin as strong founder-owned proof lanes without making unsafe or unsupported claims.

---

## 1. LumaJet safe frame

LumaJet is a simulation-first geometry/e-drive/routing/thermal/resilience proof branch within the Luma Universe.

Safe public sentence:

> LumaJet applies LumenCore geometry, FlowForm-style routing, thermal/diagnostic visibility, and resilience scoring to synthetic or controlled simulation environments so reviewers can compare geometry-informed candidates against baseline patterns.

Current internal signal language:

- LumaJet baseline benchmark activity exists.
- `LUMAJET_BASELINE_DONE` is the internal completion marker to preserve in proof notes.
- Any LumaJet win should be labeled as synthetic/simulation evidence unless externally reviewed.

Do not claim:

- aircraft certification,
- flight capability,
- propulsion construction instructions,
- autonomous physical control,
- safety certification,
- weapons capability,
- customer deployment.

---

## 2. LumaJet Proof Capsule requirements

Before LumaJet evidence is public headline material, freeze a Proof Capsule with:

- source simulation or synthetic benchmark name,
- baseline/control pattern,
- locked metric,
- run ID,
- code commit,
- input/output hashes,
- winner or result summary,
- negative results,
- limitations,
- safe public sentence.

Recommended capsule title:

> LumaJet Synthetic Geometry Baseline Capsule — `[run_id]`

---

## 3. LumaSuit / LumaSkin safe frame

LumaSuit / LumaSkin is a non-actuating smart-skin and human-performance digital twin proof branch within the Luma Universe.

Safe public sentence:

> LumaSuit/LumaSkin applies LumenCore geometry, smart-skin layout concepts, sensor ingestion, fatigue/thermal/strain scoring, haptics concepts, and human-in-the-loop safety gates to a simulation-first digital twin path.

Allowed Phase I scope:

- sensorized garment concept,
- bench sensor mesh,
- synthetic and bench data ingestion,
- fatigue/heat/strain/imbalance scoring,
- flowform/champion layout simulation,
- haptics concept review,
- mentor/lab validation plan,
- non-actuating prototype roadmap.

Do not claim:

- powered armor,
- weapons,
- autonomous actuation,
- flight control,
- medical diagnosis,
- certified safety,
- clinical validity,
- operational readiness.

---

- sensor or synthetic data source,
- baseline garment/layout/scoring model,
- locked metric,
- run or bench ID,
- hash manifest,
- risk score output,
- limitations,
- mentor/lab review request,
- next prototype gate.

Recommended capsule title:

> LumaSuit Non-Actuating SmartSkin Simulation Capsule — `[run_id]`

## 5. AR/VR and haptics boundary

AR/VR, haptics, smart-skin, digital twin, and identity-architecture language should be kept in a non-actuating, simulation-first, human-reviewed frame.

Safe wording:

> The AR/VR and haptics layer is a training and feedback concept that may support human-performance simulation, digital-twin review, and operator-facing awareness. It is not a certified safety system or autonomous physical controller.
