# EC TakeOff cohort strengths research

**Our strength is making everyone else stronger.**

LumenCore research, September 11, 2026. Public sources were reviewed September 12 UTC.

Our direction is to help other founders become easier to discover and better able to deliver what they already do well. Energy remains a major research focus. The same habit of finding a constraint, preserving service quality, measuring a baseline, and testing an improvement can also help business workflows.

This notebook covers the 69 companies on the official [TakeOff Fall 2026 roster](https://ec.co/takeoff-fall-2026-accelerator-cohort/), including LumenCore. Each entry identifies a public strength and three original questions to investigate with its owner. The linked CSV contains the same 207 questions and measurement fields.

[Download the research table](EC_COHORT_STRENGTH_RESEARCH_2026-09-11.csv)

## Evidence and participation

Company descriptions are public positioning. Proposed constraints are hypotheses; no operating data or owner interviews were used. All baseline values are unknown. This publication does not establish company participation, endorsement, measured losses, savings, grant eligibility, profitability, certification, or government approval. Proposed contributions have not been delivered as cohort packages.

Some company sites were unavailable or exposed only indexed content. Those limits remain beside each entry. Existing features are respected when suggesting a contribution. CareHome, an adjacent EC business absent from this TakeOff roster, is excluded from the 69-company count and this publication.

For any comparison, preserve all eligible work, failures, cancellations, abandoned work and unresolved cases. Report completion and quality alongside time among successful cases. Keep energy, cost, elapsed time and travel separate; incomplete work is not saved effort. No operational savings can be calculated from these unmeasured hypotheses.
