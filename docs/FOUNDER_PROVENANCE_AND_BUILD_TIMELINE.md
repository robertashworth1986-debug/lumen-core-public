## Purpose

This page shows what was built, when it became inspectable, and how founder-originated terms connect to code, tests, pull requests, manifests, and reviewer-safe artifacts.

It is a provenance index, not a biography claim, legal ownership ruling, trademark registration, patent priority determination, customer list, scientific certification, or substitute for the underlying artifacts.

## What counts as strong provenance

Evidence strength, highest first:

1. official filing, portal receipt, or signed external record;
2. Git commit with immutable SHA and dated artifact;
3. pull request with bounded scope, tests, and claim boundary;
4. hash manifest or reproducibility receipt;
5. dated document, image, notebook page, or message with intact source metadata;
6. founder recollection awaiting corroboration.

Raw chats and notebooks may establish chronology, intent, and design evolution. They do not by themselves prove scientific performance, external validation, legal priority, commercial adoption, or agency endorsement.

## Current public credibility surface

### 1. Founder origin and continuity

- dated terms and architecture families;

### 2. Engineering execution

- runnable code;

## Publication policy

Do publish:

- source title or identifier;
- date;
- SHA-256 where available;
- bounded excerpt or public-safe summary;
- linked commit, PR, manifest, or artifact;
- evidence type and claim boundary.

Do not publish:

- raw private conversation archives;
- family or personal information;
- credentials, codes, keys, or account records;
- customer or partner identities without permission;
- private portal screenshots;
- unpublished patent details or legal strategy;
- unsupported claims of first invention, exclusivity, certification, revenue, adoption, validation, or endorsement.
