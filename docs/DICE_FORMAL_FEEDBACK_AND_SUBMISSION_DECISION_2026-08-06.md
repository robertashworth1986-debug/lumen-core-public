## 3. Technical rebuild required before reconsideration

A reconsidered DICE proposal must introduce a **new, mathematically and experimentally defined control primitive**, not merely rename the existing peer-mesh framing. At minimum, it must:

1. state the foundational capability leap in one falsifiable sentence;
2. define the agent, task, communication, trust, failure, compromise, and human-control models;
3. specify the mechanism by which local interactions create useful system-level capability without central command;
4. provide testable hypotheses for scalability, adaptability, resilience, and controllability;
5. define matched baselines and ablations that isolate the proposed mechanism;
6. evaluate heterogeneous agent classes, agent loss, agent compromise, communication degradation, collusion, monitor shift, and distribution shift;
7. report raw completion, safe completion, false rejection, abstention, latency, communication cost, compute cost, and negative results;
8. separate current evidence from proposed work and retain the increased false-rejection result;
9. avoid claiming operational deployment, independent validation, TA3 conformance, CUI/classified handling, or field performance that has not occurred; and
10. explain why the result cannot be obtained by ordinary orchestration, consensus, routing, market allocation, or policy gating.

If these conditions are not met, the concept remains a research candidate rather than a competitive DICE full proposal.
