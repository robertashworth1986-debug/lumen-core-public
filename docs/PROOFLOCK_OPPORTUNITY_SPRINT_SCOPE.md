# ProofLock Opportunity Sprint — Fixed-Scope Service

## Buyer problem

A small technology firm, consultant, or research team is considering one real funding opportunity but cannot quickly determine whether it fits, what evidence belongs in the package, which facts are missing, and whether the submission effort is justified.

## Outcome

ProofLock converts that opportunity workflow into a source-grounded, preflighted, replayable decision: **pursue, revise, hold, or stop**.

## Why this is not a generic AI review

A general AI can summarize a solicitation or draft proposal language. The paid unit is the governed decision record around that assistance: locked official sources and amendments, client-confirmed facts, eligibility and authority gates, evidence-to-claim mapping, missing-fact abstention, acceptance rules, and replayable receipts. A named person is accountable for delivering the agreed artifacts. No claim is made that this method is superior until buyer pilots measure the actual workflow outcome.

## Fixed fee and timing

- Fixed fee: **$3,500 USD**
- Kickoff deposit: **50% ($1,750 USD)**
- Balance: **$1,750 USD before final handoff**
- Scope: one organization, one opportunity workflow, one decision owner
- Target delivery: ten business days after written acceptance of the scope, source permissions, baseline, acceptance rules, decision owner, and cleared deposit

Any material scope change requires a written change order before work begins.

## Included deliverables

1. Official-source and eligibility-rule register.
2. Evidence-linked pursue / no-pursue decision brief.
3. Reviewer-ready package outline.
4. Attachment, dependency, authority, and blocker ledger.
5. Claim-boundary and unresolved-fact register.
6. Replayable decision receipts for material qualification and preflight decisions.
7. Final readout with measured workflow deltas and recommended next action.

## Client inputs

- Official opportunity URL or source packet.
- Truthful entity, ownership, registration, personnel, and eligibility facts.
- Existing technical evidence, prior submissions, budgets, resumes, and attachments the client is authorized to share.
- One named decision owner.
- Written permission to inspect each non-public source.
- Timely answers to unresolved factual questions.

## Acceptance rules

The sprint is complete when the client receives all included deliverables and the decision record identifies the material sources, rules, assumptions, missing facts, blockers, package components, authority gates, and recommended pursue / revise / hold / stop outcome.

The sprint does **not** require or guarantee agency acceptance, eligibility, award, funding, legal compliance, submission, or a positive recommendation.

## Excluded and human-only actions

- Legal, tax, accounting, procurement, or investment advice.
- Certification of eligibility or factual representations.
- Signing, attesting, pricing approval, portal certification, or final submission.
- Inventing credentials, partners, performance, savings, validation, or customer commitments.
- Bulk opportunity coverage, proposal writing beyond the agreed package outline, or indefinite revisions.

The client personally retains final review, certification, signature, upload, send, and submission authority.

## Confidentiality and data handling

Only sources authorized in writing are inspected. Secrets, credentials, controlled data, legal correspondence, private identity records, and unrelated materials are excluded unless a separate written handling protocol is accepted. Public examples do not include client-confidential material.

Before any non-public source is inspected, both parties complete the
[ProofLock Opportunity Sprint Data Handling Schedule](PROOFLOCK_OPPORTUNITY_SPRINT_DATA_HANDLING_SCHEDULE.md).
It names the accepted and prohibited data classes, approved operators and
systems, external-AI/service-provider boundary, retention and deletion rules,
incident channel, and final disposition receipt. The template is not a security
certification and becomes binding only when incorporated into the controlling
written agreement.
