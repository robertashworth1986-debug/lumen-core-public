## Material Qualification Gaps

The HUD opportunity requires a real production-stage factory-built or offsite housing demonstration. The current local evidence catalog does not identify a housing manufacturer, construction operator, or robotics integrator committed to the project.

Before the application can be represented as competitive, the record needs:

- A named factory-built housing or offsite construction demonstration partner
- A robotics, automation, or AI implementation role tied to an operating production line
- A measured current-state production baseline
- Quantified targets for cost, cycle time, labor, defects, throughput, and housing affordability
- At least two recent and relevant past-performance references
- Three key personnel with credible construction, manufacturing, robotics, or demonstration experience
- A supportable three-year budget and division of work
- Letters documenting partner commitments and access to the demonstration site and data

Existing LumenCore simulations and trading-system evidence can support the orchestration, monitoring, reproducibility, and anomaly-detection portions. They cannot, by themselves, prove readiness to demonstrate home construction at production scale.
