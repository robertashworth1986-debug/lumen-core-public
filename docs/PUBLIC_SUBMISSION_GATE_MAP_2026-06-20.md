# Public Submission Gate Map

Generated: 2026-06-20

## Purpose

This map keeps the public LumenCore proof trail honest. It separates what is already visible and reproducible from what still requires portal authority, legal/compliance review, cost review, team review, or Robert's action-time approval.

The goal is not to imply that any grant has been submitted or approved. The goal is to make the remaining gates clear enough that reviewers, collaborators, and helpers can see exactly where serious support is useful.

- The public dashboard reports twenty-one portal/user gates.

- HarborSentinel has public NOAA AIS acquisition, hashes, held-out splits, full-hash split I/O preflight, and a bounded controlled-injection benchmark.

## Universal No-Click Rule

Codex/LumenCore should not upload, submit, certify, spend money, represent compliance, change legal posture, or place trades without fresh action-time approval from Robert.

This is a governance boundary, not a lack of momentum. It protects the work from accidental certifications, unsupported portal claims, and avoidable legal/compliance risk.

## Gate Families

- Portal authority: Grants.gov, BAAT, DSIP, NSF, SAM.gov, or agency workspace access must be confirmed inside the live portal before submission.
- Eligibility authority: entity, applicant role, PI role, and organization authority must be verified before certification.
- Compliance authority: CMMC, SPRS, FOCI, export-control, data-rights, and security representations must be reviewed before they are stated as facts.
- Cost authority: ROM budgets are not certified budgets; pricing, fringe, indirects, subcontractors, and cost narrative need human review.
- Team authority: partners, advisors, facilities, demo sites, and letters of support cannot be implied without written confirmation.
- Claim authority: public benchmarks can support bounded claims only; field performance, operational suitability, and agency endorsement need stronger evidence.
- Submit authority: final upload and submission require Robert's explicit action-time approval.
