## Control boundary

This matrix converts the existing commercialization/transition draft into a phase-by-phase decision tool. It does not claim a committed transition partner, government sponsor, customer, pilot, production deployment, operational authorization, field performance, revenue, or certified safety. Final solicitation terms, award documents, security requirements, data-rights clauses, and authorized-human approvals control all actual commitments.

## 4. Transition metrics and minimum evidence

### Technical transition metrics

- clean-environment installation success;
- reproducibility rate across independent runs;
- percentage of code/config/result/report artifacts covered by the manifest;
- integration time for a new agent, model, or simulator;
- unresolved interface defects by severity;
- rollback success rate and human-escalation latency;
- safe completion and constraint-violation rates;
- role-coherence length, abstention, and false rejection;
- recovery latency and recovery-message burden;
- task-concentration and selected-agent diversity;
- compromised-agent propagation and collusion success/failure rate;
- communication cost versus identical-seed baselines.

### Transition and commercialization evidence

- qualified discovery interviews with role and decision context recorded;
- written technical feedback and whether it may be cited;
- external replay attempts and discrepancy outcomes;
- validated evaluator requirements, integration constraints, and decision criteria;
- non-binding interest clearly distinguished from commitment;
- pilot, deployment, and revenue claims used only when documentary evidence exists;
- unsupported market hypotheses explicitly rejected rather than promoted as traction.

## 6. Proposal-ready transition paragraph

LumenCore will transition Coherence-Bounded Peer Mesh through a staged evidence-to-evaluation pathway. The effort will first produce a reproducible, non-actuating reference implementation and benchmark suite for sparse peer coordination, local inference-time control, failure recovery, and auditability. It will then package documented interfaces, fixed-seed runs, negative-result records, discrepancy logs, and cryptographic manifests for independent replay and authorized external simulation or test-and-evaluation integration. Progression to shadow-mode or a human-gated pilot will occur only after reproducibility, interoperability, rollback, escalation, security, and evidence-boundary gates are satisfied. Government transition categories include research laboratories, AI/autonomy evaluation teams, simulation and T&E organizations, and approved integration partners; commercial discovery will focus on AI assurance, enterprise agent orchestration, regulated multi-agent workflows, and industrial autonomy testbeds. No operational deployment, committed partner, field-validated performance, or booked revenue is claimed at the proposal stage.
