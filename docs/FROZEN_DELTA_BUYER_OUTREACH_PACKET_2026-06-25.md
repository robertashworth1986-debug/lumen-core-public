## Compliance Guardrails

For U.S. commercial email, follow FTC CAN-SPAM guidance:

- Use accurate sender and routing information.
- Use a non-deceptive subject line.
- Identify the message as commercial outreach if appropriate.
- Include a valid physical postal address.
- Include a clear opt-out path and honor opt-outs.
- Do not harvest contact lists or use misleading claims.
