Evidence boundary: LumenCore currently contains software experiments, synthetic and replay benchmarks, public-data adapters, manifests, ledgers, tests, and reviewer packets. Unless a named external party confirms otherwise in writing, these materials are not third-party validation, certification, operational deployment, guaranteed savings, or proof that one model or geometry is universally superior.

What can be inspected

  • Versioned source code and test contracts.
  • Run-specific metrics, baselines, seeds, and limitations where recorded.
  • SHA-256 manifests and chain-of-custody records.
  • Negative, incomplete, and neutral outcomes retained with positive results.

What remains unproven

  • Profitable or authorized live trading performance.
  • Universal harmonic, phase-locking, or geometry superiority.
  • Certified hardware, infrastructure, aerospace, medical, or defense performance.
  • Customer savings, agency endorsement, field validation, or licensing acceptance.

Promotion rule

A result moves forward only when its source, baseline, metric, threshold, validation window, failure rules, manifest, and claim boundary are named before promotion. Patent-sensitive implementation details remain outside this public page.
