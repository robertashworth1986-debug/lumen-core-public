04 / First commercial engagement

Buyer-Owned Baseline Validation Sprint

Initial customer focus: a utility forecasting team evaluating a change to its day-ahead load model or input pipeline. The discovery question is whether assembling a decision-ready comparison costs enough analyst time and delay to justify a focused service.

  • One authorized source and accepted incumbent baseline
  • Metric, holdout and acceptance rules agreed before scoring
  • Matched evaluation with negative findings preserved
  • Replay instructions and a reviewer-readable Proof Capsule
  • One documented next decision under buyer authority

Review effort, willingness to pay and customer savings remain to be measured. A neutral or failed result is a valid deliverable.
