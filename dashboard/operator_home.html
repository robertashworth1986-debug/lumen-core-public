Proof-backed decision infrastructure

Turn complex system claims into bounded decisions.

LumenCore packages source data, incumbent baselines, locked metrics, reproducible hashes, negative findings, and claim boundaries into one reviewer-readable Proof Capsule.

The goal is not to manufacture certainty. It is to make the next decision inspectable: reject, request information, rerun, seek external review, or define a controlled pilot.
