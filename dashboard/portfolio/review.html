From an asset to a defensible result

  1. Identify source, version, owner, dependencies and the exact claim.
  2. Freeze authorized inputs, comparator, budget, constraints and primary metric.
  3. Use appropriate holdouts, uncertainty and search controls.
  4. Preserve outputs, failures, environment and reproducible commands.
  5. Obtain independent or representative validation before operational claims.
  6. Connect accepted results to buyer-approved economics and actual terms.

Release gate: publishing a source or demonstration does not certify the research, establish customer savings or authorize production control. This review expands the documented estate without converting breadth into unsupported performance or price.
