A useful result, with its boundary

At equal conductor material, adding grid diagonals reduced mean path stretch by 8.4% but reduced mean DC conductance by 13.6%. More wire made every conductor thinner. The loop-augmented tree preserved connectivity under every single-edge deletion using fewer edges than the square grid, with lower intact conductance. This is a trade-off under declared assumptions, not a universal champion.

Scientific direction

The five Platonic solids are tetrahedron, cube, octahedron, dodecahedron and icosahedron. The founder's four Echo families are a separate conceptual taxonomy. Brachistochrone means an ideal fastest-descent cycloid. Mycelium is a fungal network. Sunflower phyllotaxis is a placement pattern. Each deserves a task-specific test.

For stronger electrical signals, freeze frequency band, material, dielectric, length or footprint, conductor budget, source and load. Compare transmission / insertion loss (S21), reflection (S11), group delay and energy. For digital links add error rate and noise. For fluid channels, use flow, pressure drop and pumping power with matched boundary conditions.
