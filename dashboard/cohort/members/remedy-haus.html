Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could campaign briefs arrive with missing brand assets or unclear approval ownership?

An intake assistant that checks required assets and drafts an explicit approval map.

What we could measure together

Measure: Clarification exchanges before a brief is production-ready (exchanges per brief).

Audit an owner-approved anonymized sample using the team's production-ready checklist.

Acceptance boundary: Creative lead verifies the brief; no invented brand facts or client endorsements.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could health-related marketing copy contain claims whose approval or source is unclear?

A claim-to-source register that flags missing support and drafts neutral alternatives.

What we could measure together

Measure: Claims lacking an approved source and named reviewer (claims per content batch).

Compare a fixed content sample with client-approved claim guidance and source material, retaining disputed items.

Acceptance boundary: No medical efficacy or compliance certification; qualified client reviewers approve all health-related content.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could reporting compare incompatible date windows or campaign naming conventions?

A deterministic report checker with an AI-written explanation that cites exact flagged rows.

What we could measure together

Measure: Reporting rows with unresolved period or identifier mismatches (rows per reporting cycle).

Reconcile the same approved aggregate exports using fixed date windows and campaign definitions.

Acceptance boundary: No causal attribution, revenue lift, or campaign changes inferred from correlations.
