Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could one lead appear multiple times or fail to link across an ad source and the client funnel?

A read-only identity reconciliation report that explains likely matches and keeps ambiguous pairs separate.

What we could measure together

Measure: Unresolved duplicate or unmatched lead records (records per fixed reconciliation batch).

Use synthetic or client-authorized deidentified exports with an agency-labeled matching reference.

Acceptance boundary: No automatic merging, ad changes, or causal claims about campaign results.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could booked, won, and collected amounts refer to different dates or definitions?

A deterministic stage and period audit with source-linked exceptions and unknown amounts retained.

What we could measure together

Measure: Stage totals that cannot be reconciled under explicit definitions (totals per client-period review).

Freeze one authorized reporting period, define each stage, and reconcile events without treating booked amounts as cash.

Acceptance boundary: Do not infer revenue, cash collection, or value from missing evidence; human approves definitions.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could alerts prioritize apparent changes caused by incomplete data rather than an actionable workflow issue?

A data-quality-aware alert explanation that shows source completeness, comparison window, and abstention reason.

What we could measure together

Measure: False or unverifiable alerts in a labeled review set (alerts per review period).

Agency reviewers label a frozen alert set with complete, late, and missing data scenarios and record missed issues too.

Acceptance boundary: No automatic campaign-budget or account action; no retention or profit improvement claim.
