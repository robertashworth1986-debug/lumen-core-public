Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could synthesizing consented audience feedback take less time without erasing minority or contradictory views?

A local redacted-note organizer that links each draft theme to supporting and contradicting passages for consultant review.

What we could measure together

Measure: Time to produce a source-traceable thematic summary (minutes/summary).

Start with 10 consecutive eligible tasks, or all tasks in one week; retain exceptions and record the sample size.

Acceptance boundary: No employee surveillance, identity inference, or individual performance scoring; suppress small identifiable groups and retain dissent.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Can a message's approved evidence and version be found quickly during review?

An approved-language register with version, source, owner, and approval status; AI can suggest draft wording only from selected approved material.

What we could measure together

Measure: Time to locate the current approved claim and rationale (minutes/review question).

Start with 10 consecutive eligible tasks, or all tasks in one week; retain exceptions and record the sample size.

Acceptance boundary: No automatic crisis response or publishing; the client and consultant make the communication decision.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could repeated workshop or diagnostic preparation become easier without flattening each client's context?

A reusable session checklist with a client-context section, existing resource references, and unresolved questions; generate a draft agenda for review.

What we could measure together

Measure: Preparation time for an accepted client-specific session (minutes/session).

Start with 10 consecutive eligible tasks, or all tasks in one week; retain exceptions and record the sample size.

Acceptance boundary: Respect proprietary frameworks and client confidentiality; do not recreate or distribute paid products without permission.
