Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Can consented waitlist feedback clarify which fit, print, and timing questions need answers before launch?

An optional owner-approved preference form and de-identified tally for fit needs, print interest, and expected availability.

What we could measure together

Measure: Qualified waitlist responses with complete preference information (percent of responses).

Start with 10 consecutive eligible tasks, or all tasks in one week; retain exceptions and record the sample size.

Acceptance boundary: Do not turn interest into orders or assumed demand; no automated marketing without the owner's approved audience and consent.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could clearer fit and care information reduce repeat questions and avoidable order corrections?

Draft a product-specific size and care FAQ from approved measurements and instructions, with missing fields flagged.

What we could measure together

Measure: Fit/care questions requiring individual staff handling (questions/eligible inquiry).

Start with 10 consecutive eligible tasks, or all tasks in one week; retain exceptions and record the sample size.

Acceptance boundary: Never invent garment dimensions or care claims; retain unresolved fit questions for a person.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Can made-to-order production promises be tracked without creating excess inventory commitments?

A small order-status sheet linking approved order, material availability, production slot, and promised date; hypothetical examples until real orders exist.

What we could measure together

Measure: Accepted orders due by the observation cutoff completed within their confirmed production window (percent of all accepted orders due by cutoff).

Include every accepted order due in the frozen observation window; separately report on-time completed, late completed, overdue open, and cancelled orders with reasons. Do not remove failed commitments from the denominator.

Acceptance boundary: Owner controls commitments and artist rights; do not assume current revenue or production capacity from the roster's stage label. Retain overdue and unfinished orders; do not improve the percentage by dropping cancellations or uncompleted orders. Report missing promised dates and do not hide them by excluding those orders from the service review.
