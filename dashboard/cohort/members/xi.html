Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could clearer size and product information reduce repeated pre-purchase questions?

A catalog completeness worksheet highlights missing owner-supplied measurements, materials and care notes.

What we could measure together

Measure: Repeated size or specification questions per product inquiry (questions/inquiry).

Freeze eligible work, observation window and acceptance criteria with the owner; retain incomplete work and exceptions.

Acceptance boundary: Do not invent measurements or fit guarantees; the owner approves all product facts.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Where do stock records disagree with items available to promise across selling channels?

Reconcile authorized inventory exports and physical counts while separating holds, returns and damaged stock.

What we could measure together

Measure: Inventory discrepancies per counted SKU (discrepancies/SKU).

Freeze eligible work, observation window and acceptance criteria with the owner; retain incomplete work and exceptions.

Acceptance boundary: No automatic purchasing or stock adjustment; retain exceptions and avoid unsupported demand forecasts.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could a consistent return-reason record reveal preventable product-page confusion?

Classify owner-approved, anonymous return reasons and propose one factual content correction to test.

What we could measure together

Measure: Returns linked to incorrect or missing product information (returns/review window).

Freeze eligible work, observation window and acceptance criteria with the owner; retain incomplete work and exceptions.

Acceptance boundary: Do not infer customer attributes or suppress legitimate returns; compare margin and satisfaction with volume.
