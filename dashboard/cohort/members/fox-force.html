Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could a program-readiness checklist make instructor, venue and participant communications easier to coordinate?

A fictional event board tracks instructor availability, venue needs, materials and communications ownership.

What we could measure together

Measure: Planned events with all owner-defined readiness items confirmed (percent).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: No tactical instruction, participant risk scoring or automatic communications.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Would explicit entity tags help keep administrative records routed to the correct organization?

A synthetic record template separates foundation and company program ownership and records unresolved allocations.

What we could measure together

Measure: Sample administrative records assigned to an owner-confirmed entity (percent).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: No legal, tax or accounting allocation decisions.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could an aggregate program-evidence template simplify preparation of partner reports?

A report worksheet links aggregate participation, delivered sessions and participant-consented feedback to source records.

What we could measure together

Measure: Report entries supported by approved attendance and activity records (percent).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: No invented impact, safety outcomes or fundraising claims; preserve missing feedback.
