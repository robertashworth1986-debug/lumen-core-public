Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could participants' audience, occasion, and speaking constraints require repeated clarification before a lesson plan is prepared?

A structured speaking brief and draft lesson outline using instructor-approved exercises.

What we could measure together

Measure: Clarification rounds before an approved coaching brief (rounds per brief).

Review anonymized intake examples against the instructor's required brief fields.

Acceptance boundary: Instructor approves the plan; do not infer personality, disability, or psychological traits.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could rehearsal feedback vary because the evaluation criteria are not explicit in each review?

A transcript-based feedback draft citing exact passages and instructor-defined criteria.

What we could measure together

Measure: Feedback items that cannot be linked to an approved rubric (items per rehearsal review).

Have the instructor score consented or synthetic practice samples and audit feedback against the same rubric.

Acceptance boundary: Speaker consents to recording; instructor verifies feedback; no career or confidence outcome claim.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could participants miss the appropriate practice resource after a session?

A lesson-to-resource checklist with opt-in follow-up drafts and current links.

What we could measure together

Measure: Assigned resources without a traceable delivery or acknowledgement record (resources per session sample).

Inspect an approved administrative sample for assigned resource, correct version, and recorded delivery status.

Acceptance boundary: No automatic sending or retention of sensitive rehearsal content.
