Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could product facts diverge between labels, product pages, and support answers?

A source-linked content comparison that flags differences and drafts neutral corrections for review.

What we could measure together

Measure: Conflicting factual product statements (statements per approved content audit).

Compare current surfaces against an owner-approved label and fact sheet; record uncertain or unsupported statements.

Acceptance boundary: Do not generate health efficacy, dosage, safety, or regulatory claims; qualified owner review is required.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could subscription support questions require repeated lookup of current terms?

A retrieval-only FAQ assistant that cites the exact applicable policy and prepares a staff-reviewed draft.

What we could measure together

Measure: Time to draft an accurate subscription-policy response (minutes per response).

Use fictional support cases and the current approved terms, recording errors and missing-policy abstentions.

Acceptance boundary: No account access, cancellation, charge, refund, or automatic sending.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could inventory decisions be difficult when subscription demand and one-time orders are recorded differently?

A read-only unit reconciliation and scenario worksheet that labels assumptions and missing data.

What we could measure together

Measure: Unreconciled order units across sales channels (units per reporting period).

Compare authorized aggregate orders, subscription schedules, and inventory records for one fixed period.

Acceptance boundary: No automatic reorder, demand guarantee, or projected savings claim; owner approves operational decisions.
