Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could each active product idea be tied to one specific user task and a recorded piece of customer evidence?

A product decision sheet that records intended user, job to be done, evidence source, next uncertainty, and the owner's next decision.

What we could measure together

Measure: Active ideas with an explicit user task and supporting evidence (percent of active ideas).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: Do not invent a product, customer interview, demand, or revenue; fewer active ideas is not automatically success.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

For an existing product, can its operating effort and vendor spend be related to completed, accepted user tasks?

A spreadsheet joining owner-supplied bills and task counts for one product, with shared costs and missing allocations shown separately.

What we could measure together

Measure: Owner-recorded operating cost per accepted user task (USD/accepted task).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: Do not estimate margins from unknown bills, omit failed/repeated tasks, or treat dollar cost as energy consumption.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could product feedback lead to clearer decisions if every report identifies the affected product and version?

A local feedback register that groups duplicates while preserving original reports, affected version, evidence, and disposition.

What we could measure together

Measure: Feedback items linked to product, version, and an owner decision (percent of feedback items).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: Use consented or synthetic examples; no automatic product changes and no invented user impact.
