Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could product descriptions stay consistent with an owner-approved evidence and wording register?

A SKU-to-approved-copy register with source, version, reviewer, and unresolved questions; AI highlights discrepancies for qualified review.

What we could measure together

Measure: Product-copy statements missing an approved source or reviewer (percent of checked statements).

Start with 10 consecutive eligible tasks, or all tasks in one week; retain exceptions and record the sample size.

Acceptance boundary: Do not generate medical, absorption, safety, deficiency, or treatment claims; marketing testimonials are not efficacy evidence.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could order preparation catch product or quantity mismatches before shipment?

A local order-to-pack checklist using approved SKU identifiers, quantity, and batch fields where those already exist.

What we could measure together

Measure: Owner-confirmed pick/pack discrepancies (discrepancies/100 orders).

Start with 10 consecutive eligible tasks, or all tasks in one week; retain exceptions and record the sample size.

Acceptance boundary: No purchase, fulfillment action, or batch-safety decision is automated; protect customer details and preserve actual exceptions.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could routine order-support answers be drafted faster from current store policies?

A source-linked helper limited to shipping, returns, order status, and other owner-approved store information.

What we could measure together

Measure: Draft-and-review time for a non-medical support response (minutes/response).

Start with 10 consecutive eligible tasks, or all tasks in one week; retain exceptions and record the sample size.

Acceptance boundary: Escalate health, reaction, and product-use questions to the existing qualified process; no personalized supplement recommendations.
