01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could a partner-handoff checklist make responsibility for each administrative next step clearer?

A fictional referral-event table tracks sending organization, receiving organization, acknowledgment and agreed next step.

What we could measure together

Measure: Program handoffs with a confirmed organizational owner and next step (percent).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: No criminal-history records, participant ranking or eligibility decisions.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Would a training-delivery evidence checklist simplify preparation of a funder or agency report?

A template links session dates, aggregate attendance counts and approved materials to owner-entered reporting requirements.

What we could measure together

Measure: Contracted session deliverables with complete approved evidence (percent).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: No fabricated attendance, certifications or participant outcomes.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could a consistent engagement brief reduce repeated clarification before a consulting session?

A brief builder records organization goals, audience, session format, dependencies and explicit exclusions.

What we could measure together

Measure: Clarification rounds before an engagement scope is accepted (rounds/engagement).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: Drafts only; no contract commitments or government representations.
