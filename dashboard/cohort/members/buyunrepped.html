Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Would an intake-completeness check reduce broker time spent chasing missing property or buyer-provided details?

A synthetic transaction checklist validates the presence of owner-selected intake fields and leaves uncertain entries blank.

What we could measure together

Measure: Intakes complete before the broker's first review (percent).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: No drafting legal terms, deciding offer price or giving representation advice.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could a date-source ledger make transaction reminders easier to verify before they are sent?

A review-only deadline table stores the source, date, timezone and confirming person for each milestone.

What we could measure together

Measure: Proposed reminders with a confirmed source document and date (percent).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: No automatic legal deadline inference or messages; broker review is mandatory.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Would one explicit transaction status reduce repeated administrative updates across participants?

An owner-defined status template distinguishes awaiting information, broker review, offer-ready and coordination stages.

What we could measure together

Measure: Staff minutes spent answering duplicate status inquiries (minutes/transaction).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: Do not contact counterparties or imply an offer has been submitted or accepted.
