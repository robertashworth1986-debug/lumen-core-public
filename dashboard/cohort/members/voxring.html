Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Which scheduling exceptions most often need human correction after an assistant interaction?

Replay synthetic time-zone, cancellation, rescheduling and duplicate-request cases against an expected calendar outcome.

What we could measure together

Measure: Incorrect or conflicted bookings per reviewed scheduling attempt (exceptions/attempt).

Freeze eligible work, observation window and acceptance criteria with the owner; retain incomplete work and exceptions.

Acceptance boundary: Use test calendars only; retain failed attempts in the denominator and require owner-approved success rules.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could clearer escalation rules improve completion when the assistant is uncertain or outside its approved scope?

Score synthetic call scenarios against a sourced answer set and explicit transfer conditions.

What we could measure together

Measure: Unresolved eligible interactions and inappropriate automated answers (cases/review window).

Freeze eligible work, observation window and acceptance criteria with the owner; retain incomplete work and exceptions.

Acceptance boundary: Never replace clinical triage or emergency handling with the test tool; no call recording without authorization.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Where do repeated webhook events or delayed sync create inconsistent call, lead and appointment records?

Reconcile test event IDs and timestamps across authorized export samples, including retries and out-of-order arrivals.

What we could measure together

Measure: Cross-system mismatches per completed interaction (mismatches/interaction).

Freeze eligible work, observation window and acceptance criteria with the owner; retain incomplete work and exceptions.

Acceptance boundary: No outbound calling or live CRM writes; marketing accuracy and security claims on the site remain unverified.
