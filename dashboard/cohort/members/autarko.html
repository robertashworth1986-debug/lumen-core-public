Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could a readiness checklist reduce missing information before a hardware-and-role configuration quote?

An intake validator records approved roles, data sources, access scope and hardware constraints before a human configuration review.

What we could measure together

Measure: Quote requests needing a second requirements round (percent).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: No hardware purchasing or customer-system access.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Would a source-freshness test help keep policy-based agent drafts tied to the correct document version?

An offline fixture suite mixes current, superseded and missing policy excerpts and checks citations and abstentions.

What we could measure together

Measure: Draft answers correctly citing the owner-approved current policy (percent).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: Use synthetic policies; no approval or autonomous task execution.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could a measured workload comparison identify whether an agent role adds useful work within an agreed resource budget?

A measurement worksheet compares the existing process with one SOVR role, retaining quality, elapsed time, overhead and uncertainty separately.

What we could measure together

Measure: Whole-system energy per correctly completed and approved task (Wh/task).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: No energy-saving claim from CPU time, token counts or modeled power; qualified measurements are required.
