Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could research evidence be reused across an engagement without losing its source or context?

Index owner-approved public research and redacted interview notes; draft a brief with a source reference beside each factual statement.

What we could measure together

Measure: Time to assemble a source-checked buying-group brief (minutes/brief).

Start with 10 consecutive eligible tasks, or all tasks in one week; retain exceptions and record the sample size.

Acceptance boundary: Keep contradictory evidence and distinguish source facts from interpretation; no invented interviews or buying roles.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Do differing client funnel definitions make reporting unnecessarily slow to reconcile?

A client-owned metric dictionary and CSV mapping validator that flags incompatible definitions before a report is generated.

What we could measure together

Measure: Reporting records needing manual definition reconciliation (percent of records).

Start with 10 consecutive eligible tasks, or all tasks in one week; retain exceptions and record the sample size.

Acceptance boundary: Do not manufacture attribution, substitute proxy activity for revenue, or silently merge unlike stages.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could a client operate delivered marketing systems with fewer clarification requests after handoff?

Generate a draft operating guide and acceptance checklist from the actual configuration and approved decisions, with links to client-owned assets.

What we could measure together

Measure: Client support contacts during the first agreed handoff period (contacts/accepted handoff).

Start with 10 consecutive eligible tasks, or all tasks in one week; retain exceptions and record the sample size.

Acceptance boundary: Client validates access, ownership, and task completion; no claim of autonomous readiness until a real handoff test passes.
