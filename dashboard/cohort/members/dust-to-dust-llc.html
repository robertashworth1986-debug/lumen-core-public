Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could an approved-information library make early public inquiries easier to answer consistently?

A source-linked FAQ draft template separates the proposed service, current availability and questions requiring the owner's response.

What we could measure together

Measure: Inquiry drafts answered entirely from approved source text (percent).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: No claims about legal availability, environmental performance or disposition advice.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Would a dependency checklist clarify which readiness items must be resolved before any launch commitment?

A local readiness map tracks facility, vendor, operating-procedure and authorization questions as unresolved until evidence is supplied.

What we could measure together

Measure: Owner-defined launch dependencies with named evidence and responsible person (percent).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: This is a planning aid, not a permit, legal determination or launch authorization.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could explicit capacity assumptions help compare possible service designs before investment?

A scenario sheet separates intake demand, cycle duration, facility capacity and staffing assumptions without defaulting unknowns to zero.

What we could measure together

Measure: Scenario inputs supported by an owner-provided measurement or clearly labeled assumption (percent).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: No purchase advice or claims of achievable throughput; owner specialists must confirm inputs.
