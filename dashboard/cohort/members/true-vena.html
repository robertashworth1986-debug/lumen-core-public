Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could an appointment's administrative prerequisites be checked with less back-and-forth before a visit?

A synthetic or de-identified scheduling checklist that records whether required documentation and contact logistics were verified by authorized staff.

What we could measure together

Measure: Visits needing an administrative correction before scheduling (percent of visits).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: Do not collect patient information in the demonstration, interpret test orders, supply preparation instructions, or decide clinical suitability.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Can route planning fit visit windows and lab handoff deadlines without adding unnecessary travel?

A read-only route worksheet using synthetic locations first, then only owner-approved operational data and fixed pickup/handoff constraints.

What we could measure together

Measure: Logged travel distance per completed comparable mobile visit (miles/visit).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: Qualified staff preserve specimen handling, timing, and care requirements; no dispatch changes, clinical prioritization, or fuel-saving claim from route estimates.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could staff locate the administrative status of a specimen handoff more quickly?

A synthetic status-event log with anonymous job ID, timestamp, responsible role, and acknowledgement, highlighting missing administrative events.

What we could measure together

Measure: Time to resolve a handoff-status inquiry (minutes/inquiry).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: No patient results, diagnoses, or replacement for the authorized laboratory chain-of-custody record; do not infer specimen integrity.
