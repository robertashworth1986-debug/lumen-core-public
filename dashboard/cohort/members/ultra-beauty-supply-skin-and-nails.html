Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Can early customer conversations clarify the product types and pack sizes professionals most need locally?

An owner-approved discovery worksheet and anonymous demand tally to distinguish specific professional needs from general interest.

What we could measure together

Measure: Confirmed product requests containing the agreed purchase details (percent of requests).

Start with 10 consecutive eligible tasks, or all tasks in one week; retain exceptions and record the sample size.

Acceptance boundary: No assumed orders, projected revenue, or unauthorized outreach; validate supplier availability before promising stock.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could preparing a product catalog take less time with a consistent supplier-data format?

A CSV validator for supplier SKU, brand, pack size, unit, price, source date, and approved product description; draft missing-data requests.

What we could measure together

Measure: Catalog preparation time per verified SKU (minutes/SKU).

Start with 10 consecutive eligible tasks, or all tasks in one week; retain exceptions and record the sample size.

Acceptance boundary: Do not invent ingredients, certifications, product compatibility, or supplier authorization; prices and availability need owner verification.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Can education and product inquiries be organized without repeated manual sorting?

Tag voluntary inquiries as availability, product details, professional education, or order support, then draft a response from approved material.

What we could measure together

Measure: Time to route an inquiry to the correct owner-approved response (minutes/inquiry).

Start with 10 consecutive eligible tasks, or all tasks in one week; retain exceptions and record the sample size.

Acceptance boundary: No skin diagnosis or treatment advice; keep beauty-product education separate from personalized healthcare.
