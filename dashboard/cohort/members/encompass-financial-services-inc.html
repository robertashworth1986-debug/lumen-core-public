Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

If the existing practice is in scope, could a statement-completeness grid reduce repeated document requests?

A synthetic checklist maps accounts and reporting periods to received-document indicators, without account numbers or balances.

What we could measure together

Measure: Client-months with all owner-required source statements before preparation (percent).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: Owner must confirm venture scope; do not collect tax identifiers or real bank documents.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

If the existing practice is in scope, would an exception-only reconciliation view reduce review effort?

A local synthetic-ledger checker flags missing source references, possible duplicates and unmatched entries for accountant review.

What we could measure together

Measure: Reviewer minutes per owner-confirmed reconciliation exception (minutes/exception).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: No automatic journal entries, tax classifications or financial advice.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

If the existing practice is in scope, could report-source links make month-end review easier to reproduce?

A report checklist records period boundaries, source versions and reviewer sign-off using fictitious amounts.

What we could measure together

Measure: Report figures traceable to approved period and source records (percent).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: No tax filing, payroll execution or assertion that accounts are correct.
