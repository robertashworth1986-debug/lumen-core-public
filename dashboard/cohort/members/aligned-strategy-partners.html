Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could one intake brief make the choice between partner-led support and guided tools clearer?

A brief builder summarizes stated goals, available implementation time and requested support against published service descriptions.

What we could measure together

Measure: Intakes requiring clarification before owner-approved service routing (percent).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: Route recommendations remain editable and owner-confirmed; no invented client budget.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Would a dependency-and-owner checklist make a client's next implementation step easier to identify?

A small roadmap validator flags missing owners, blocked prerequisites and undefined completion evidence.

What we could measure together

Measure: Open roadmap steps without an owner or prerequisite (count).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: Use the existing roadmap; do not create a parallel project system.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could a before-and-after time diary distinguish implementation benefits from changes in client workload?

A baseline worksheet records repeated tasks, volume, exceptions and observed time before and after one agreed workflow change.

What we could measure together

Measure: Owner administrative minutes per comparable completed service unit (minutes/unit).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: No claimed time or financial savings until comparable observations exist.
