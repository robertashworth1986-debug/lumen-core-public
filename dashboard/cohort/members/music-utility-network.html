Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could users choose an agreement workflow before the relevant transaction facts are clear?

A factual intake checklist that presents candidate workflow categories and asks for missing information.

What we could measure together

Measure: Intake cases requiring manual reclassification (cases per reviewed intake set).

Use fictional scenarios labeled by an authorized contract professional and score the current intake flow.

Acceptance boundary: No legal advice or determination that an agreement is suitable; authorized professional approves.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could missing fields or obsolete template versions be overlooked before review?

A deterministic template completeness checker with source-linked explanations of flagged fields.

What we could measure together

Measure: Incomplete or outdated drafts reaching professional review (drafts per review batch).

Inspect a fixed synthetic set for required fields, version identifiers, and conflicting names or dates.

Acceptance boundary: Do not assess fairness, enforceability, ownership, or legal compliance; no signing or submission.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could contributors have difficulty seeing which documents or approvals remain outstanding?

A read-only document-status table that links each status to a dated receipt or marks it unknown.

What we could measure together

Measure: Status discrepancies between the checklist and source documents (discrepancies per project review).

Reconcile a fictional rights-document packet with the current administrative status tracker.

Acceptance boundary: Never infer rights clearance from missing evidence; no outreach or contractual action.
