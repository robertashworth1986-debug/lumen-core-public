Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could preparing a property research packet take less time while preserving traceable source dates?

A public-source research checklist and document index for parcel facts, comparable data, and dated assumptions; AI drafts a summary with citations.

What we could measure together

Measure: Time to prepare a source-complete property packet (minutes/property).

Start with 10 consecutive eligible tasks, or all tasks in one week; retain exceptions and record the sample size.

Acceptance boundary: Do not present an automated value as an appraisal; retain conflicting records and licensed-data restrictions.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Would explicit units and assumption versions reduce spreadsheet review corrections?

A read-only spreadsheet intake checklist that records units, periods, source dates, debt assumptions, and scenario labels before human review.

What we could measure together

Measure: Material formula, unit, or assumption corrections found in a review (corrections/model).

Start with 10 consecutive eligible tasks, or all tasks in one week; retain exceptions and record the sample size.

Acceptance boundary: No automatic purchase, investment advice, syndication solicitation, or claimed return; a qualified owner reviews the full model.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could due-diligence requests be closed with fewer searches across document versions?

A local diligence index with document version, requested item, accountable owner, and status; draft missing-item reminders for review.

What we could measure together

Measure: Time to locate the current evidence for a due-diligence request (minutes/request).

Start with 10 consecutive eligible tasks, or all tasks in one week; retain exceptions and record the sample size.

Acceptance boundary: Do not distribute investor information or private documents; unresolved legal and transaction questions remain with the responsible professional.
