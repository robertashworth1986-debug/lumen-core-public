Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could a shared inquiry taxonomy make it easier to direct a request to the correct venture?

A review-only intake map classifies the request type against the studio's published venture descriptions.

What we could measure together

Measure: Inquiries reassigned after initial routing (percent).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: Do not infer financial needs or send messages automatically.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Would a common definition sheet make cross-venture reporting easier to reconcile?

A metric dictionary records owner, period, numerator, denominator and source for each selected operational measure.

What we could measure together

Measure: Reported metrics with an agreed definition and source (percent).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: Do not combine incomparable audiences, revenues or periods.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could a source-and-approval checklist shorten preparation of reusable executive and media content?

A content brief links approved evidence and reuse rights to each planned article, talk or client explanation.

What we could measure together

Measure: Revision rounds caused by missing sources or approvals (rounds/item).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: No fabricated citations, investment recommendations or automatic publication.
