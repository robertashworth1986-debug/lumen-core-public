Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could a project-to-cost-code check identify handoff fields that need review before billing preparation?

A synthetic export validator checks project IDs, approved work references and cost-code completeness.

What we could measure together

Measure: Approved work items with a valid owner-confirmed cost-code link (percent).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: No payroll, billing or accounting system writes.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Would an explicit document-version register reduce ambiguity during submittal review?

A small manifest compares document IDs, revision labels and approval status while preserving superseded versions.

What we could measure together

Measure: Review tasks linked to the confirmed current document revision (percent).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: Do not declare drawings safe or approved; the responsible reviewer confirms status.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could an RFI timeline audit make delayed handoffs easier to explain?

A local event checker finds missing owners, impossible event order and elapsed-time outliers in fictional RFI records.

What we could measure together

Measure: RFIs with complete sent, assigned and response timestamps (percent).

Agree eligible work, observation window, quality threshold and exclusions with the owner before comparing.

Acceptance boundary: No inferred contractual deadlines or automated notices.
