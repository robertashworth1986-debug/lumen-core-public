Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could compatible visits be grouped to reduce travel without shortening care or breaking appointment windows?

Compare the current visit sequence with owner-approved area groupings using anonymous visit IDs.

What we could measure together

Measure: Travel time per completed visit (minutes/visit).

Freeze eligible work, observation window and acceptance criteria with the owner; retain incomplete work and exceptions.

Acceptance boundary: Keep every care commitment and animal-handling constraint; the owner approves any schedule change.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Which missing intake details cause repeat clarification before a concierge, matching or training appointment?

A service-specific completeness checklist distinguishes required care facts from optional preferences.

What we could measure together

Measure: Clarification rounds before a complete intake (rounds/intake).

Freeze eligible work, observation window and acceptance criteria with the owner; retain incomplete work and exceptions.

Acceptance boundary: Do not infer veterinary conditions or pet suitability from a short form; qualified staff make care decisions.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could a consistent after-session summary reduce repeated instructions while preserving individual training plans?

Organize trainer-approved notes and next-step checklists by anonymous session ID.

What we could measure together

Measure: Staff time preparing an approved session follow-up (minutes/session).

Freeze eligible work, observation window and acceptance criteria with the owner; retain incomplete work and exceptions.

Acceptance boundary: Use only trainer-approved content and consented communication; measure correction rate alongside time.
