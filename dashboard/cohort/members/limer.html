Three questions worth exploring

PUBLIC RESEARCH

These are hypotheses to discuss, with current baselines unmeasured. They do not establish that a problem or loss occurs in this business.

01OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could pop-up stock, samples, and returned units require manual reconciliation?

A small inventory reconciliation sheet with anomaly flags and a documented adjustment log.

What we could measure together

Measure: Unexplained unit difference after an event (units per event).

Reconcile starting units, sales, samples, damage, and returns for existing event records without imputing missing counts.

Acceptance boundary: Owner approves inventory adjustments; no sales or waste reduction is assumed.

02OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could retailer or sampling inquiries arrive without the information needed for a useful response?

A draft wholesale intake checklist and approved-product-information reply assistant.

What we could measure together

Measure: Clarification steps before an approved wholesale response (steps per inquiry).

Review an anonymized sample for quantity, delivery area, timing, and requested product information.

Acceptance boundary: No automatic pricing commitments, outbound messages, or retailer orders.

03OWNER INPUT NEEDED

Could packaging, retail sheets, and website copy drift from the approved product facts?

A document comparison tool that flags ingredient, size, storage, or wording differences for review.

What we could measure together

Measure: Fact mismatches across approved product surfaces (mismatches per review).

Owner defines the current product fact sheet, then compares a fixed set of labels and descriptions.

Acceptance boundary: Do not infer hydration or health efficacy; only owner-approved product facts enter drafts.
