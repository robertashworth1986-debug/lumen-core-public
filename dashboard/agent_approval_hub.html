Operating Boundary

This hub is deliberately conservative. It is built to increase decision quality, auditability, and reviewer trust. It does not represent that the trader is institution-ready, profitable, or safe for unmanaged live execution. Those claims require forward-tested, cost-adjusted, leakage-free evidence.
