Why not just ask an AI?

AI can draft. ProofLock governs the decision.

A general AI can summarize a solicitation or suggest prose. It does not automatically establish which source version controls, which private facts are verified, which claims have evidence, which attachments and authorities are missing, or whether the final record can be replayed after the chat ends.

Safe first contact

Start with the public opportunity link—not your confidential files.

For the fit check, send the official public source, deadline, organization name, decision owner, and the decision that is blocked. Do not email credentials, financial-account details, government identity numbers, protected health information, classified/CUI or export-controlled material, privileged legal communications, or confidential technical evidence.

Before we inspect any non-public source, both parties name the approved operators, systems, external AI or service providers, retention and deletion rules, incident channel, and final disposition receipt in writing.
