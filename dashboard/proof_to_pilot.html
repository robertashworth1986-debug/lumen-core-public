The customer problem

A team has a promising result, but procurement, a funder, or a technical reviewer cannot tell whether it beats the incumbent under locked rules—or whether the evidence was tuned, filtered, or promoted after the outcome was known.

Freeze before scoring

Source rights, baseline, primary metric, threshold, holdout, failures, and allowed claims are named first.

Run matched conditions

Candidate and baseline use the same authorized source and evaluation contract. Neutral and failed results stay visible.

Return one bounded decision

The output is promote, rerun, external review, hold, or reject—not a broad promise that the technology works everywhere.
