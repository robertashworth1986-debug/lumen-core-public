# LumaJet Technical Abstract

## Title

LumaJet: Geometry-Inspired Aerospace Digital Twin for Autonomy Assurance, Thermal Routing, and SBIR Phase I Evidence Generation

## Problem

Early aerospace concepts often fail review because they jump from vision to vehicle claims without traceable requirements, controlled baselines, repeatable tests, safety boundaries, and third-party validation paths.

## Proposed innovation

LumaJet applies LumenCore champion geometry, honeycomb modular layout, flowform routing, phase-coherence scoring, and proof freezing to an aerospace-style digital twin. The purpose is to evaluate whether geometry-guided routing and modular layout strategies can outperform simple baseline layouts in simulation.

## Phase I objective

Build a simulation-only LumaJet proof stack that:

1. Generates candidate geometry patterns.
2. Compares champion flowform routing against straight-grid and random baselines.
3. Produces traceable JSON, CSV, Markdown, and SHA256 manifests.
4. Maps outputs to aerospace-style evidence categories.
5. Identifies what is proven, what is modeled, and what still requires lab or mentor validation.

## Claim boundary

This is not a flight-ready system, not an aircraft design package, not drone control, and not certification evidence. It is a Phase I digital-twin and proof-readiness package.
